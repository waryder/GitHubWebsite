New AI Models on OpenRouter

  • Thinking Machines: Inkling 2 Mini (thinkingmachines/inkling-2-mini, listed 2026-11-21 02:19 UTC) — open-weight multimodal looped transformer, 3.4B active of 26.4B total parameters (MoE) with middle layers run three times, 1,048,576-token context, text+image input. $0.10 per million prompt tokens and $0.40 per million completion tokens, with cached input at $0.02.
  • RadixArk: DaisyRose 27B (radixark/daisyrose-27b, listed 2026-11-21 00:49 UTC) — open-weight multimodal reasoning model trained from Qwen3.8 27B using reinforcement learning only on long-horizon agent tasks, with no supervised fine-tuning or distillation. 262,144-token context, text+image input. $0.15 per million prompt tokens and $1.88 per million completion tokens.
