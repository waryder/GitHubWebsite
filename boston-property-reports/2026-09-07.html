STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — the 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum, permit cap declining 1,100→900 by 2026, $375/$750 fees) remains under an active preliminary injunction from the October 2024 Superior Court order in Narragansett 2100 v. Town of Narragansett (WC-2024-0372) and is not being enforced. The most recent confirmable checkpoint is a law-firm alert updated 5/11/2026 showing no trial, settlement or appellate ruling; no harder September-2026 docket confirmation was reachable with web tools. The “duplex STR ban” this report has carried appears to be a conflated premise — the Town’s own STR Q&A implies duplex units are rentable, each requiring separate registration, and the “ban” language traces to an unrelated April 2024 proposal restricting new duplex construction in certain zones. Flagged as unresolved rather than corrected. Sources: Town Q&A, Partridge Snow & Hahn.
  • Warwick RI — STR is a permitted use in all residential zones plus the Village mixed-use zone. Barred for ADUs, affordable/deed-restricted units, and properties with nuisance or code violations. $250/unit permit, Fire Marshal inspection, state DBR registration. No blanket ban and no numeric cap. Source: City of Warwick memo.
  • North Kingstown RI — no dedicated STR ordinance or registry found; handled ad hoc through general zoning and building review. Source: Steven Miller Group.
  • South Kingstown RI — registry adopted 4/8/24 (Ch. 9 §§9-200–210), $100 fee, registration deadline extended to 3/31/26, fines to $500. The town’s own page was JS-gated; terms corroborated from secondary sources.
  • Jamestown RI — Council approves STRs individually and administratively and can impose caps at its discretion. As of Feb 2024 there were 64 locally approved against 170 state-registered, implying a large grey market. Source: Jamestown Press.
  • Barrington RI — Ordinance Ch. 185 Art. XXXIII (2022): occupancy capped at 2 per bedroom, parking ratios, police registration, 24/7 contact required, and ADUs barred from STR use. No blanket ban or numeric cap. Source: eCode360.
  • Warren RI — terms as confirmed 2026-09-06 from local reporting: a 125-unit townwide cap, a 50%-of-building-units cap, $100/unit annual fee with a $50 homestead discount, one off-street parking space per unit (Council-waivable), and Fire Marshal inspection authority. A status check found no 2026 news of amendment, repeal or challenge, so the ordinance appears current — but the EastBayRI page that closed this blackout last run returned blank on fetch this run, so this run’s confirmation is snippet-level only. Municode was not re-fetched, per the source-class rule.
  • Statewide — RI 5% whole-home STR tax CONFIRMED. Effective 1/1/2026 per RI Division of Taxation Advisory ADV 2025-16; applies to whole-home rentals of 30 days or fewer and stacks on the existing 7% sales tax plus a local hotel tax that rose from 1% to 2% the same date. Sources: ADV 2025-16, What’s Up Newp.
