Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — the 2024 ordinance set a declining permit cap (1,100 in 2024 → 1,000 in 2025 → 900 by 2026) plus a 7-night minimum stay. Still under the October 2024 Superior Court preliminary injunction (Judge Sarah Taft-Carter, Narragansett 2100 v. Town) blocking enforcement of the cap while litigation continues; the town extended registration to 2025-02-24 at $120/unit as a stopgap. No ruling newer than that was found this run — treat the cap as still enjoined and unenforced, and verify with the town before relying on it. Boston Globe · WPRI · Patch
  • North Kingstown RI — no dedicated STR registry ordinance found; the town appears to rely on existing zoning and special-use permitting (confirm with Building/Zoning). The statewide ban on renting ADUs for transient stays applies. South County STR guide
  • South Kingstown RI — Short-Term Residential Rental Registry adopted 2024-04-08 (Town Code Ch. 9, §§9-200–210). $300/unit registration, $300 late fee, in-unit posting of local ordinances required. Town of South Kingstown
  • Jamestown RI — registration mandatory since 2023-08-07: $350/yr, building plus fire marshal inspection (re-inspection every 3 / 2 years), occupancy of 2 per bedroom +2, 1 parking space per bedroom +1, RI-resident local contact with 2-hour response, and revocation after 3 violations in 12 months. The council retains authority to cap totals townwide or by neighborhood. Jamestown Press
  • Warwick RI shore (Oakland Beach, Conimicut, Buttonwoods, Warwick Neck) — comprehensive ordinance passed February 2023; STRs are a permitted use in residential and mixed-use zones, with safety-equipment requirements, 180-day guest-record retention and noise/nuisance rules. Amended to drop the owner-occupancy requirement. Warwick Post
  • Barrington RI (duplex footprint) — registration with the Police Department, 24/7 local contact, occupancy of 2 per bedroom, 1 parking space per 2 guests, 3-year occupancy-record retention; ADUs banned from transient use. eCode360
  • Warren RI (duplex footprint) — annual registration required before occupancy (fee reported around $100/unit for some owner categories — confirm the exact schedule with the town), Fire Marshal/Chief inspection authority, occupancy capped by permit. The council has moved toward a townwide unit cap plus parking mandate; whether a final cap ordinance passed was not confirmed this run. Municode · EastBayRI
  • Statewide RI 5% whole-home STR tax — re-confirmed in effect. Effective 2026-01-01, Rhode Island imposes a 5% tax on residential dwellings rented in their entirety for stays of 30 days or less, keyed to date of occupancy rather than booking date. It is distinct from, and does not stack with, the existing 5% state hotel tax on single-room stays; the local hotel tax also rose from 1% to 2%. RI Division of Taxation FAQ · RI Association of Realtors · Sales Tax Institute
