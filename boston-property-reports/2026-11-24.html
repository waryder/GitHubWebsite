STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — 2024 permit-cap ordinance (7-night minimum; cap 1,100 → 1,000 → 900 from Sept 2026) remains enjoined (TRO/preliminary injunction 2024-10-02, Narragansett 2100 suit); Aug and Sept 2026 coverage reports the case still ongoing with no final judgment (AirHostd, Phipps Realty) — blog sources; docket not read. Town rental registration ($120/unit, due Aug 31) still applies.
  • South Kingstown RI — STR registration ordinance adopted 2024-04-08; enforcement suspended Nov 2024 after the Narragansett injunction (town notice); current status unclear.
  • Jamestown RI — registration required for <30-day rentals: $125 license plus per-bedroom fee, fire and building inspections (Jamestown Press).
  • North Kingstown RI — no dedicated STR registry; regulated via zoning/building/assessor; STR use can affect homestead tax status.
  • Warwick RI — no STR cap found; not confirmed this run.
  • Statewide RI — DBR registration; 5% whole-home STR tax on ≤30-day platform rentals effective 2026-01-01, local hotel tax on STRs 1% → 2% (RI Division of Taxation FAQ); trafficking-prevention training for operators (RI Realtors).
