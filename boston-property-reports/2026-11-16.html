  • North Kingstown RI — no town zoning ban found; town registration listed as "to be confirmed"; state DBR registration required (strprofitmap, secondary).
  • Barrington RI, Warren RI — not researched this run.
  • Statewide — 5% whole-home STR tax and local hotel tax rising 1%→2%, effective 2026-01-01 (RI Realtors).
