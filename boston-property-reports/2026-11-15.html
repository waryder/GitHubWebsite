STR rules by town

  • Statewide RI: from 2026-01-01 a 5% whole-home STR tax on stays ≤30 days, local hotel tax raised 1%→2% (combined ~14% with 7% sales tax); $25/yr DBR registration shown on listings; fines $250/$500/$1,000; ADUs barred from STR use since 6/25/2024. (RI Realtors)
  • Narragansett RI: the 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum, permit caps 1,100/1,000/900, $1M insurance) was preliminarily enjoined 2024-10-02 (Narragansett 2100 v. Town, decision). Latest secondary source (Air Hostd, 2026-08-24) says the injunction still stands; no final merits ruling, appeal outcome or repeal found. STRs currently fall under general rental registration ($120/unit/yr). Unresolved — confirm with the Building Dept before buying.
  • South Kingstown RI: $300/unit/yr registration for rentals ≤30 days; no ban found.
  • Jamestown RI: no cap or minimum stay now, but compliance certificate, Town Clerk registration and Town Council license (apply by Oct 1) required, plus per-bedroom fees ($200/bd for non-resident owners); a staff proposal restricting non-hosted rentals to commercial districts is under review — material risk for a non-resident buyer. (bnbcalc)
  • Warwick RI, North Kingstown RI, Warren RI, Barrington RI: no current STR rule found this run — check directly.
