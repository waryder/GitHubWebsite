Short-term-rental rules by town

  • Statewide RI. Any STR listed on a hosting platform must register with the RI Dept. of Business Regulation before listing (R.I. Gen. Laws §42-63.1-14); the registration number and expiration must appear on the listing. Accessory dwelling units are statutorily barred from STR/transient use (§45-24-73). RI DBR
  • Statewide 5% whole-home STR tax — confirmed still in effect. Effective 2026-01-01 and verified live as of this run against the RI Division of Taxation's own December 2025 FAQ and January 2026 coverage. Stacked with the existing 7% sales tax and the local hotel tax rising from 1% to 2%, whole-home STRs now face roughly 14% combined lodging tax. RI DoT FAQ · RI DoT notice · RI Realtors · WhatsUpNewp
  • Narragansett RI — re-checked this run, still unresolved. The 2024 ordinance (Ch. 1123) imposes a 7-night minimum stay, a declining annual permit cap (1,100 → 1,000 → 900 for the year beginning September 2026), and a ban on weddings, parties and corporate events. It remains under court injunction: Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted a TRO and preliminary injunction in October 2024 in the Narragansett 2100 home-rule challenge, and the most recent development locatable is a stay reported around December 2025, with the court expecting the town to return with further justification. No final ruling, trial outcome or resolution could be found between then and August 2026. This is a live, fluid legal situation — confirm current status directly with the Town of Narragansett solicitor's office before underwriting any Narragansett RI purchase on the permit-cap framework. Boston Globe · WPRI · GoLocalProv · Town registration page
  • South Kingstown RI (covers Wakefield RI, Peace Dale RI, Matunuck RI). Annual Short-Term Residential Rental registration required under Town Code Ch. 9 §§9-200–9-210: $100 fee, $300 late fee, deadline extended to March 31, 2026. Ordinances, lease and registration form must be posted inside the unit. No permit cap, no injunction — the cleanest regulatory environment among the focus towns. Town notice
  • North Kingstown RI (incl. Wickford RI, Saunderstown RI). No dedicated townwide STR registry; handled case-by-case through zoning, building and assessor review. State DBR registration still mandatory. Confirm with Building/Zoning before buying — and note that STR use can affect homestead tax-exemption status.
  • Warwick RI. Dual registration: state DBR ($25/yr, effective Jan 30, 2025) and a city permit (Owner's Affidavit, a local contact who must respond within 30 minutes, annual fire/building inspection). Zoning permits STRs in residential and mixed-use village zones only, not commercial or industrial. Fines to $500/day; two or more violations can revoke the permit. City of Warwick
  • Jamestown RI. Local registration via OpenGov, annual Oct. 1 deadline, $350/yr, biennial fire-marshal and building inspection. Occupancy capped at 2 per bedroom + 2 (children under 12 excluded), 1 parking space per bedroom + 1, local representative must respond within 2 hours, 3 violations in 12 months revokes. As of 2026, 126 STRs licensed with 6 pending — a functioning, uncapped system with no litigation found. Jamestown Press
  • Bonnet Shores RI. A Narragansett RI neighborhood — governed entirely by the Narragansett rules above, injunction included.
