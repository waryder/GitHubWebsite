Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — ordinance still enjoined, litigation unresolved. The 2024 ordinance (Ch. 1123) would cap STR permits (1,100 declining to 900 by fall 2026) and impose a 7-night minimum. Narragansett 2100 sued; Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted injunctive relief in October 2024 on the grounds that the town did not follow Zoning Enabling Act procedure. As of August 2026 the injunction still stands and no resolution date has been announced — the town continues registering under the prior ordinance (Sec. 14-518 to 14-520). Boston Globe · WPRI · The Independent
  • South Kingstown RI (Wakefield, Peace Dale, Matunuck) — registry adopted April 2024, Town Code Ch. 9 §§9-200–9-210. $300/unit/yr for rentals of 30 days or less (capped at $1,000/owner), $50/unit for 30+ day rentals, posting requirements for noise, parking and occupancy rules. No cap on permit numbers found. Town of South Kingstown
  • North Kingstown RI — no dedicated STR ordinance found; regulated only through existing zoning, building code and assessor homestead rules. The least restrictive of the towns checked but also the least documented — confirm directly with NK Building/Zoning before purchase. The Independent
  • Jamestown RI — registration required since January 2024, tiered by residency ($100/bedroom for resident non-occupant owners, $200/bedroom for non-residents), $350 annual inspection fee, occupancy capped at 2 per bedroom excluding children under 12, and cesspool properties barred from STR use. Jamestown Press
  • Warwick RI — STR permitted with registration through the online portal. ADUs, accessory structures, affordable-housing units and code-violation properties are barred from STR use; occupancy is 2 per bedroom plus 2; guest records must be retained 180 days. No numeric cap found. City of Warwick
  • Statewide RI tax — confirmed still in force. Effective 2026-01-01, a new 5% state tax applies to whole-home short-term rentals on top of the existing hotel tax, and the local hotel tax doubled from 1% to 2%. Combined lodging tax load is now roughly 14%. Liability is based on the date of occupancy, not booking. No repeal found as of August 2026. RI Division of Taxation Notice 2025-09

The two above are the documented cases; smaller unnamed colonies in the same corridor were not surfaced by this pass.
