STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — 2024 ordinance enjoined: 7-night minimum, STR permits, inspections, $1M liability, local rep, permit caps 1,100 → 1,000 → 900 (9/2026). RI Superior Court (WC-2024-0372) granted a TRO and preliminary injunction on 10/2/2024; a third-party summary dated 8/24/2026 describes litigation as ongoing with no final judgment found. Existing rental registration still applies ($120/unit, due 8/31; $300 late fee). Court decision · AirHostd summary. Re-check with the Town before buying.
  • Warwick RI — registration by Dec 31 ($250/unit), local rep, inspections, 2 persons/bedroom max, permitted only in certain zoning districts, fines $500/day.
  • North Kingstown RI — registration $100/yr (Dec 31), registered agent; STR registration forfeits the Homestead Exemption.
  • South Kingstown RI — no STR ordinance as of 12/2023; current status unverified this run.
  • Jamestown RI — registration by Oct 1 ($700 non-owner-occupied / $350 owner-occupied), inspections every 2 years, local rep.
  • Barrington RI — ≤30-day rentals regulated; local rep, 2 persons/bedroom.
  • Warren RI — $100/unit registration, inspections; multifamily may not devote more than 50% of units to STR.
  • Statewide — 5% RI whole-home STR tax (stays of ≤30 days) effective 2026-01-01; platforms collect, direct-booking owners must register. RI Division of Taxation FAQ.

Town rules other than Narragansett RI come from the RI League of Cities & Towns 12/2023 analysis (PDF) and may be dated.
