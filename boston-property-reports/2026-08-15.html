STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — 2024 permit-cap ordinance (cap 1,100→900, 7-night minimum) remains ENJOINED and unenforced. Superior Court TRO + preliminary injunction in place since Oct 2024 (procedural defect under the Zoning Enabling Act; 7-night minimum conflicts with state law). Latest dated reporting (Dec 2025 council session / Jan 2026 legal summary): injunction still in effect, case unresolved, town exploring re-enactment as a zoning-ordinance amendment. No 2026 ruling or re-enactment found as of 2026-08-15 — do not assume the cap is binding, but a zoning re-do could arrive with limited notice. Sources: Boston Globe, Steven Miller Group
  • South Kingstown RI — Rental Registration ordinance adopted 2025-10-14: $300/unit/yr for STR (≤30-day stays), $50/unit long-term, $300 fine for non-registration; enforcement active.
  • Jamestown RI — functioning STR regime: annual registration (Oct 1 deadline), state DBR registration first, biennial inspections, occupancy/parking caps, 2-hour local-responder rule. 126 STRs licensed in 2026. Jamestown Press
  • Warwick RI — STRs permitted in residential/mixed-use village zones (2023 ordinance), prohibited in commercial/industrial zones; guest-record keeping (180 days) and safety-equipment rules. guide
  • North Kingstown RI — no townwide STR registry found; case-by-case via zoning/building; check before listing.
  • Statewide RI — 5% whole-home STR tax in force since 2026-01-01 plus local hotel tax now 2% (combined ~14% stack); mandatory DBR registration with number on listing; ADUs may not be STRs; new 2026 annual human-trafficking-prevention training requirement. RI Div. of Taxation

  • Rate benchmarks: AirDNA figures blend all stay lengths — directional only; single confirmed agency weekly rate (Lila Delman, 20 Lane 2, Narragansett RI).
  • Drive times and hospital/library/gym proximities are estimates from general town geography, not routed calculations, except where a listing itself cites a distance (38 Dean St, Rehoboth MA: 6.9 mi to Attleboro station).
  • Planet Fitness: confirmed clubs at Stoughton MA (638 Washington St) and Norwood MA (418 Walpole St); no standalone Canton MA or Sharon MA club found — nearest are Stoughton/Norwood MA (~10–15 min est.).
  • Norwood MA rail note: Norwood is on the Franklin Line, not Providence/Stoughton; nearest in-scope stations are Canton Junction / Sharon MA (~10–15 min est.).
