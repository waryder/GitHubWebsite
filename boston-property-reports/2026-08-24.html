Short-term-rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — still enjoined, litigation unresolved. The 2024 ordinance banned stays under 7 nights and phased permit caps down to 900 by September 2026. Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted a preliminary injunction in October 2024 on the grounds the ordinance likely conflicts with and is preempted by state law; the town extended registration deadlines in February 2025 rather than enforcing the ban. As of this run, no merits ruling, trial date or appellate decision newer than 2025 was found — the injunction appears to remain the operative status. Boston Globe · Partridge Snow & Hahn · The Independent
  • North Kingstown RI — no dedicated townwide STR ordinance found; relies on state DBR registration. The Independent
  • South Kingstown RI — registry adopted April 2024: $100/yr fee, $300 late fee, annual December 31 renewal, posted-notice requirements. Town of South Kingstown
  • Jamestown RI — registration required since August 2023: $350/yr, building and fire inspections every 3 and 2 years, two people per bedroom cap, cesspool properties excluded, local representative must respond within 2 hours. Jamestown Press
  • Warwick RI — February 2023 ordinance: STR permitted in residential and mixed-use zones, banned in commercial and industrial; annual state DBR plus city registration, annual inspection, local contact reachable within 30 minutes, in-law apartments and affordable units excluded, two-per-bedroom occupancy cap, fines up to $500/day. City of Warwick · BNBCalc
  • Barrington RI — police-department registration, 24/7 contact required, two guests per bedroom, one parking space per two guests. eCode360
  • Warren RI — annual town registration; an STR is any stay under 28 consecutive days in a residential zone. Checkmate Rentals
  • Rhode Island statewide 5% whole-home STR tax — re-confirmed in effect. Effective 2026-01-01, applied to whole-dwelling short-term rentals based on date of occupancy, separate from the existing 5% state hotel tax (never both on the same stay). The local hotel tax also rose from 1% to 2%. RI Division of Taxation notice · Sansiveri, August 2026 update
