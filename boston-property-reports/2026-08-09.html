Short-term rental rules by town

  • Statewide RI — the new 5% whole-home short-term-rental tax took effect 2026-01-01, alongside the local hotel tax doubling from 1% to 2%. Combined lodging tax on a whole-home STR now reaches roughly 14%. No single stay is subject to both the 5% hotel tax and the 5% whole-home tax. All taxes apply by date of occupancy, not date of booking. RI Division of Taxation notice · Sales Tax Institute
  • Narragansett RI — re-checked this run, still unresolved. The 2024 ordinance capping STR permits (1,100 for the year beginning September 2024, stepping down to 900 for the year beginning September 2026) remains under a Rhode Island Superior Court preliminary injunction issued by Judge Sarah Taft-Carter in October 2024, sought by the property-owner group Narragansett 2100. The litigation is still active with no final ruling found, and the cap is not currently being enforced. Because the case could resolve at any time, verify with the town clerk or town solicitor before committing capital in Narragansett RI. Boston Globe · WPRI · Town Q&A
  • South Kingstown RI — short-term residential rental registry adopted April 2024, broadened by a Rental Registration ordinance adopted 2025-10-14. Fees are $300 per unit for rentals of 30 days or less and $50 per unit (capped at $1,000 per owner) for longer terms. The registration deadline was extended to 2026-03-31. Town of South Kingstown RI
  • Jamestown RI — registration required since 2023-08-07: $350/yr flat, or $100–$200 per bedroom depending on residency and capped at four bedrooms. Building inspection every three years, fire marshal every two, occupancy capped at two per bedroom, and short-term rental is prohibited outright on properties served by a cesspool — check the septic record before offering on either Southwest Ave unit. Regulation guide
  • Warwick RI — STR has been a permitted use in residential and mixed-use village zones since February 2023 and is prohibited in commercial and industrial zones. Requires safety equipment, guest record-keeping retained 180 days, and compliance with noise and nuisance rules. As of 2026-01-01 RI's Human Trafficking Prevention Notice and Training Act adds an annual operator training requirement. City of Warwick RI
  • North Kingstown RI — no dedicated townwide STR registry or ordinance page was located this run; the town appears to regulate through existing zoning, building code and assessor homestead-tax rules rather than a specific STR permit scheme. Confirm directly with Building/Zoning before purchase. Town of North Kingstown RI
