STR rules by town

  • Statewide RI: DBR registration required before listing on any hosting platform, with the registration number and expiration shown on the listing. ADUs may not be short-term rented (R.I. Gen. Laws §45-24-73). Effective 2026-01-01: a new 5% statewide whole-home STR tax on entire-dwelling stays of 30 nights or fewer, plus the local hotel tax raised from 1% to 2% — these stack with the existing 5% state hotel tax and 7% sales tax on gross rental receipts. Sources: RI Division of Taxation FAQ, RI REALTORS®.
  • Narragansett RI — 2024 permit-cap ordinance, re-checked this run: the Town Council's 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum stay; permit cap stepping down from 1,100 to 900 by September 2026) was blocked by Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter, who granted a TRO and preliminary injunction on 2024-09-18 and reaffirmed the block in an October 2024 ruling. The most recent reporting located (November 2025) still describes the ordinance as on hold amid the lawsuit, with Narragansett 2100 v. Town of Narragansett unresolved — no 2025 or 2026 final decision was found this run. Practical read for a buyer: the cap is not currently being enforced, but it is a live legal risk that could flip against short-term rentals with little notice. Sources: Boston Globe, The Independent, town rental-registration page.
  • South Kingstown RI (incl. Wakefield RI, Matunuck RI): annual Short-Term Residential Rental registration required — $100 fee, $300 late fee, fines to $500, plus posting and occupancy-notice requirements. Town page.
  • North Kingstown RI (incl. Wickford RI, Saunderstown RI): no dedicated townwide STR ordinance or registry; managed through zoning, building, and assessor rules. Short-term leasing can affect the homestead tax exemption. Source.
  • Warwick RI: February 2023 ordinance — STRs are a permitted use in residential and mixed-use village zones and prohibited in commercial and industrial zones; safety equipment mandatory, owner/LLC identification required. WarwickPost, Warwick Beacon.
  • Jamestown RI: registration mandatory since August 2023 — $350 annual fee, building-official inspection every 3 years and fire-marshal inspection every 2 years. Regulation guide.
