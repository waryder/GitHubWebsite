Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — cap still enjoined. The 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum; permit cap declining from 1,100 to 900 by September 2026) remains under the October 2024 Superior Court preliminary injunction issued by Judge Sarah Taft-Carter. No 2026 ruling superseding it was found this run — status unchanged. Confidence: high. Boston Globe · WPRI
  • Narragansett RI duplex ban — re-confirmed never adopted. The 14-amendment zoning package that would have restricted duplex construction town-wide was withdrawn by the Town Council in May 2025 after resident opposition at public hearing. Confidence: high, though the primary local article sits behind a paywall and this rests on corroborating search summaries. The Independent
  • Warren RI — ordinance text unfetchable for a FOURTH consecutive run. Municode again returned only a JavaScript site shell with no ordinance text. The reported terms remain consistent across two independent EastBayRI articles: a 125-unit townwide cap, a 50%-of-units-per-building rule for 2–5 unit buildings, a $100/unit annual fee with a $50 resident discount, and a mandatory off-street parking requirement. Status stays high confidence, primary text unconfirmed. Recommendation: this needs a call to Warren Town Hall, not a fifth scrape attempt. EastBayRI
  • Warwick RI — re-confirmed, not re-litigated. Warwick's own zones are A-40 / A-15 / A-10 / A-7; "R-6" and "R-10" are West Warwick's codes and belong to a different municipality. Zoning Ordinance §510 and the city Planning Department's 2023-10-11 memo to the state STR commission establish that STR is permitted by right in residential and Village mixed-use zones. Nothing contradicting this surfaced this run. Confidence: high. Residual caveat unchanged: per-parcel zoning has never been individually verified, and the Oakland Beach / Conimicut cottages are very likely A-7 but should be confirmed before purchase.
  • North Kingstown RI — re-confirmed: no STR ordinance at all. No registry and no Municode STR chapter; owners are directed to Building/Zoning for case-by-case special-use guidance. Confidence: medium-high (this is an absence of ordinance rather than an affirmative town statement). South County STR overview
  • South Kingstown RI — two-track rules re-confirmed. Locally, a Short-Term Residential Rental Registry adopted 4/8/2024 (Town Code Ch. 9, §§9-200–9-210) requires registration of all STRs. Separately and independently, the statewide ADU bar at R.I. Gen. Laws §42-63.1-14, effective 6/25/2024, prohibits accessory dwelling units from being listed on hosting platforms for stays under 30 days regardless of local registration. Confidence: high. Town of South Kingstown
  • Rhode Island statewide 5% whole-home STR tax — re-confirmed from the primary source. Effective 1/1/2026, a 5% tax applies to whole-home short-term rentals of 30 days or less, on top of the 7% sales tax and a local hotel tax raised from 1% to 2%. It is distinct from the 5% state hotel tax on single-room rentals, and the two never stack on the same stay. Confidence: high — primary notice fetched. RI Division of Taxation Notice 2025-09
