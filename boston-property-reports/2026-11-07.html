STR rules by town

  • Rhode Island statewide: 5% state tax on whole-home STRs (30 days or less, platform-listed) effective 2026-01-01; local hotel tax raised 1%→2%; DBR registration and annual human-trafficking-prevention training required (RI Realtors).
  • Narragansett RI: 2024 ordinance (permit cap 1,100/1,000/900, 7-night minimum, 29-night maximum) enjoined by RI Superior Court in Oct 2024 (Narragansett 2100 Inc. v. Town of Narragansett, WC-2024-0372); Town called it "essentially suspended" in Dec 2024 and rescission failed 2-2. No later ruling, appeal or repeal found — current status UNCONFIRMED; litigation unresolved. Building Dept 401-789-1044 x2211. Weekly stays would still fit a 7-night minimum. Bonnet Shores RI falls under Narragansett plus association rules (not retrieved).
  • South Kingstown RI: April 2024 registration ordinance, not enforced after the Narragansett ruling (Nov 2024); later council amendments possible — unconfirmed.
  • Per a Dec 2023 RI League of Cities analysis (may be outdated): Jamestown RI registration $350/$700; Warwick RI $250/unit and limited to certain zoning districts; North Kingstown RI $100/yr (forfeits Homestead Exemption); Barrington RI registration, 2 persons per bedroom; Warren RI $100/unit, multifamily may use at most 50% of units as STRs. No outright bans found; primary ordinances not read.
