Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI (including Bonnet Shores) — injunction still unresolved, ordinance still NOT enforced. The 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum stay, separate STR permit, $1M liability insurance, permit cap declining 1,100 → 1,000 → 900 through 2026) remains under a preliminary injunction as of the most recent confirmable reporting, August 2026. Superior Court Justice Sarah Taft-Carter blocked it in October 2024 in a suit by Narragansett 2100, Inc. and landlords arguing a home-rule and state-law conflict. No final ruling, settlement, or appellate decision has been reported through 2025–2026. A general (non-STR) rental registration still applies: $120/unit/yr, due Aug 31. Boston Globe · WPRI · Aug 2026 update
  • South Kingstown / Wakefield / Peace Dale RI — registration required (Town Code §§9-200–9-210, adopted April 2024). STR defined as under 30 days. $100/yr fee due Dec 31, $300 late fee, violation fines to $500. No permit cap found. Town of South Kingstown
  • North Kingstown / Wickford / Saunderstown RI — ordinance exists (§9-420 et seq.) but Municode would not fully render; the town was deliberating a local registry as of 2023 (54 state-registered units then) and relies on RI DBR registration in the interim. No confirmed cap or minimum stay. Municode
  • Jamestown RI — registration since August 2023: $350/yr, fire and building inspection on a 2–3 year cycle, local contact required with 2-hour response, permit revocable after 3+ violations in 12 months. 64 STRs operating as of February 2024. No cap or minimum stay. Jamestown Press
  • Warwick RI shore — STRs permitted by right in single-family zones plus one village district. Requires RI DBR registration ($25/yr since January 2025) plus a $250/yr city permit, annual inspection, 30-minute local-contact response, and occupancy capped at 2 per bedroom. A 2023 proposal to restrict Oakland Beach to owner-occupied-only STRs was reported but no confirmation was found that it passed — treat as likely not enacted, and re-check before relying on it. City of Warwick · WarwickPost
  • Bonnet Shores RI — not a separate municipality; governed by Narragansett's rules above.
  • Statewide 5% RI whole-home STR tax — CONFIRMED IN EFFECT. Verified directly against two primary RI Division of Taxation documents. Effective January 1, 2026, a 5% Whole Home Short-Term Rental Tax applies to whole-dwelling rentals of 30 days or less — houses, condos and mobile homes, exactly the target property types — on top of the existing 7% sales tax, and the local hotel tax rose from 1% to 2% the same date. Combined stack: 7% + 5% + 2% = 14% on rental charges. Notice 2025-09 · Whole Home Tax FAQ
