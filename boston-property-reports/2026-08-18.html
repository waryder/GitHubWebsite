STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — the 2024 ordinance caps STR permits, stepping down from 1,100 (2024) to 900 (2026), with a 7-night minimum stay. It has been under a RI Superior Court injunction since October 2024 (Judge Sarah Taft-Carter, Narragansett 2100 v. Town Council). The most recent reporting located is December 2025, when the judge issued a stay expecting the town to justify the ordinance further; no 2026 merits ruling was found as of 2026-08-18. Treat enforcement as suspended and uncertain. Sources: Boston Globe, WPRI, South Kingstown Insider.
  • South Kingstown RI (incl. Wakefield and Matunuck) — registry adopted April 2024. $300/unit for stays of 30 days or less, $50/unit above that, capped at $1,000/owner; noise, parking and trash rules must be posted. Active, not in litigation. Town notice.
  • North Kingstown RI (incl. Wickford and Saunderstown) — no dedicated townwide STR ordinance or registry found; zoning, building and assessor rules apply, and STR use may affect the homestead tax exemption. Confirm with NK Building/Zoning before buying. The Independent.
  • Jamestown RI — registration required since August 2023: $350/yr, building and fire-marshal inspections on 3-year and 2-year cycles, occupancy capped at 2 per bedroom plus 2, and the town may deny "for just cause". Jamestown Press.
  • Warwick RI (Oakland Beach, Conimicut, Buttonwoods, Warwick Neck) — February 2023 ordinance permits STR in residential and mixed-use zones and bans it in commercial/industrial; annual inspection, $25/yr state DBR registration, and a local contact who can respond within 30 minutes. Warwick Building Dept.
  • Statewide RI — effective 2026-01-01 the local hotel tax rose from 1% to 2% and a new 5% whole-home STR tax was added, so combined lodging tax on whole-home STRs can reach up to 14% (7% state sales tax + 2% local hotel + 5% whole-home). Tax is based on occupancy date, not booking date. Confirmed in effect. RI Division of Taxation notice, Sales Tax Institute, Avalara.

Coverage & Caveats

  • Norwood Hospital re-confirmed closed. Norwood Hospital, 800 Washington St, Norwood MA has had no ER since the June 2020 flood. Reconstruction stalled in February 2024 amid Steward Health Care's collapse and the shell is reported roughly 75% complete. Governor Healey signed legislation in early August 2026 authorizing DCAMM to take the property from owner Medical Properties Trust by eminent domain and convey it to a nonprofit operator, with MPT reportedly given until 2026-11-01 to sell voluntarily. No operator and no reopening date. The nearest confirmed open EDs are Milton Hospital, Good Samaritan Medical Center in Brockton MA, and Brigham & Women's Faulkner in Boston MA — all an estimated 10–20 min depending on origin town, none verified per listing. Sources: Boston.com, WBUR, Boston Globe. Note the signing date is reported as 2026-08-07 by WBUR against the 2026-08-04 figure carried in earlier runs — treat early August 2026 as the confirmed window.
  • Norwood MA rail flag. Norwood MA is served by the MBTA Franklin/Foxboro line (Norwood Central, Norwood Depot), not the Providence/Stoughton line. The nearest Providence/Stoughton station to a Norwood address is Canton Junction at an estimated 10–15 min, which exceeds the 10-minute rule. Both Norwood MA rows are flagged accordingly.
  • Blocked and gated sources. Redfin was gated for listing detail across all three research passes and returned only neighborhood boilerplate. LandWatch listing pages render client-side and produced no verifiable per-listing URLs. Zillow behaved inconsistently: its detail pages were readable for the RI pass but its search UIs were gated in the northern MA corridor, and Land.com's town search pages returned "0 Results" under raw fetch so only individually-linked property pages were usable. Homes.com, LandSearch, Raveis, Boston Pads, LandVest, Hogan Associates and MNT Intl rendered server-side and carried most of this run.
  • Estimated unconfirmed inventory: ~30–40% for the Canton/Stoughton/Sharon/Norwood MA cluster, ~15–20% for the Mansfield/Attleboro/Rehoboth MA cluster, and ~35–45% across the RI sets. Those are gaps in source access, not confirmed absences.
  • Utilities. Public water/sewer-at-street status was not disclosed on any Section 1 listing page fetched this run — every parcel is marked unconfirmed rather than guessed, and nothing was dropped for it. Rehoboth MA is a rural town generally on well and septic.
  • Zoning. Zoning notes come from listing remarks only; none were cross-checked against town bylaws or GIS this run. Treat as directional.
  • Drive times. Every station, hospital, library and Planet Fitness figure in this report is an estimate based on general geography, not a live routing query.
  • 38 Dean St, Rehoboth MA appears in both Section 1 and Section 3 because Homes.com categorizes it as multi-family while the listing description reads as a single dwelling plus barn. It is flagged unconfirmed in both, consistent with prior-run treatment.
  • Rate benchmarks are directional, not underwriting-grade. Only Narragansett RI has a solid third-party occupancy/ADR figure. Warwick shore, Jamestown RI, North Kingstown RI and Bonnet Shores RI weekly-rate data are gaps requiring a live AirDNA pull or a local agency.
