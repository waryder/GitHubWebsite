Short-term-rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — the 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum; permit cap starting at 1,100 and dropping to 900 by September 2026) remains under the preliminary injunction granted by Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter in October 2024, with the judge indicating landlord group "Narragansett 2100" has a strong case. As of January 2025 the town still ran STR registration ($120/unit) despite the injunction. No source newer than January 2025 was found this run despite repeated searching — the injunction's continued validity today is inferred, not freshly confirmed. Re-check the RI Superior Court docket (WC-2024-0372) or local press before relying on it. This is the single most important item to verify before advising a buyer.
  • South Kingstown RI — registry ordinance adopted April 2024 (Ch. 9, §9-200–210); annual registration $100 ($300 late fee), fines up to $500. No cap, no ban.
  • North Kingstown RI — Short-Term Rental Registration ordinance (Municode Art. XVIII, §9-420 et seq.); no cap or ban identified.
  • Jamestown RI — permitting system since 2023/2024; state law bars outright bans so none exists. Prohibits STRs on properties with cesspools; biennial inspections required; ~170 registered rentals.
  • Warwick RI — February 2023 ordinance: STR is a permitted use in residential and mixed-use zones, banned in commercial and industrial; $250/unit annual registration; guest records retained 180 days.
  • Barrington RI / Warren RI — no STR-specific ordinance found in this pass; these towns were in scope for the duplex section only and were not verified further.
  • Statewide RI 5% whole-home STR tax — re-verified in effect today. Effective 2026-01-01, RI imposes a 5% tax on whole-home short-term rentals (occupancy-date based, distinct from the existing 5% single-room hotel tax), and the local hotel tax doubled from 1% to 2% the same date. Sources: RI Division of Taxation FAQ, Sales Tax Institute, What's Up Newp.
