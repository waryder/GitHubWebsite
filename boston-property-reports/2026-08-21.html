Criteria
  • Section 1 — corridor land & homes: 0.75–5.0 acres inclusive; vacant/buildable land, teardown-grade structures, or existing single-family homes. Public water AND sewer at street preferred but never disqualifying. ≤10 min to an MBTA Providence/Stoughton line station, ≤15 min to a hospital, ≤30 min to a highly rated library, ≤30 min to a Planet Fitness. Towns: Canton, Stoughton, Sharon, Norwood, Mansfield, Attleboro and Rehoboth MA. The manufactured-home requirement is dropped from this section. Rehoboth MA fails the 10-minute rail rule at ~18–22 min and is included anyway, flagged, because it carries the deepest qualifying inventory.
  • Section 2 — beach-area weekly-rental buys: properties to buy and then rent by the week. Condos, duplexes, mobile/manufactured homes and small cottages — not estates. Near beaches and within ~15 min of a Providence/Stoughton line station, in areas where weekly vacation renting is already established.
  • Section 3 — dwelling-unit duplexes: two separate living units with private entrances, across the full Sections 1+2 footprint plus Barrington and Warren RI. No acreage cap, no price cap, no minimum room count.
  • All drive times are estimates. Every listing carries a real source URL. Each run is a fresh search — no day-over-day deltas are computed.

STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — ordinance WC-2024-0372 (Ch. 1123): the permit cap (1,100 falling to 900 by 2026) and 7-night minimum stay remain under a preliminary injunction. Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted the injunction in October 2024 on the view that the ordinance’s advertising ban likely conflicts with state law. No 2026 merits ruling was found in this run — Narragansett 2100 v. Town of Narragansett appears still pending, with the town operating under the older Section 14-518–520 registry rather than the capped ordinance. Treat as unresolved and verify with the town before any purchase. WPRI · Boston Globe
  • Rhode Island statewide — 5% whole-home STR tax: confirmed in effect since 2026-01-01 on whole-home and whole-condo stays of 30 nights or less, alongside the local hotel tax rising from 1% to 2% on the same date. Combined state and local load on whole-home short stays reaches roughly 13–14% depending on jurisdiction. It does not stack with the single-room 5% hotel tax — the two are mutually exclusive. RI Division of Taxation notice · Avalara
  • South Kingstown RI: annual STR registration required — $100 fee, $300 late fee, fines up to $500.
  • Warwick RI: STRs are a permitted use in residential and mixed-use zones under the 2023 ordinance, with registration, safety-equipment and guest-record requirements. Roughly 73 registered units as of the most recent count, with known active operators in Oakland Beach.
  • Jamestown RI: existing ordinance covers roughly 87 registered STR units.
  • North Kingstown RI (including Wickford and Saunderstown RI): no dedicated STR registry. Governed by general zoning, building and assessor rules; short-term leasing may affect the homestead exemption. Confirm with the town before buying.
  • Statewide ADU restriction: RI Gen. Laws §45-24-73 prohibits listing an accessory dwelling unit for tourist or transient use on hosting platforms. This matters for any “in-law suite” cottage or second-structure property — notably 2185 Post Rd, Wakefield RI in Section 3.
