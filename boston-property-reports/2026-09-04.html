Short-term rental rules by town

  • Statewide RI — 5% whole-home STR tax, effective 2026-01-01. Re-confirmed from the primary source. Applies to entire homes/condos rented ≤30 days, separate from the existing 5% state hotel tax on single-room rentals (no stay pays both). The local hotel tax also doubled from 1% to 2% on the same date. All of it keys to date of occupancy, not booking date. RI Division of Taxation notice · advisory.
  • Warwick RI — the two-run "R-6/R-10" contradiction is RESOLVED, and it was a town mix-up. Prior runs carried a claimed R-6/R-10 STR bar that Warwick's own 2023 memo appeared to contradict. Searching Warwick's zoning text directly, "R-6" and "R-10" do not appear anywhere in it — those codes belong to West Warwick, a separate municipality. Warwick's actual single-family zones are A-40 / A-15 / A-10 / A-7 (the number is minimum lot size in thousands of sqft). Zoning Ordinance §510 states STR "shall be permitted by right in certain zoning districts as presented in Table 1," and the city's Planning Department memo to the state STR commission says plainly that STR was added as a permitted use within the City's residential and Village (mixed use) zones and is not permitted in commercial or industrial zones. This is material: five Section 2 candidates and five Section 3 duplexes are in Warwick RI. Caveat: per-parcel zoning was not individually verified — the Conimicut cottages are very likely A-7 but confirm before purchase. Warwick STR memo (primary PDF) · West Warwick zoning map confirming R-6/R-10 is theirs.
  • Narragansett RI — permit cap still enjoined, still no 2026 ruling. Judge Sarah Taft-Carter's October 2024 injunction against the permit-cap and 7-night-minimum ordinance remains in force; Narragansett 2100 v. Town of Narragansett is still open. The ordinance is on the books but unenforceable pending trial — which is not the same as void, so a buyer should verify permit standing rather than assume freedom. The proposed duplex ban is re-confirmed WITHDRAWN (May 2025) and has not been re-adopted. Boston Globe · WPRI.
  • Warren RI — cap now corroborated by two independent articles, but STILL not primary-source confirmed. Last run reported the cap as adopted at second reading c. 2023-02-21 on the strength of search-indexed EastBayRI text. This run found two separate EastBayRI stories, pre-vote and post-vote, independently and consistently describing the same terms: a 125-unit townwide cap, a 50%-of-units-per-building cap (a 2-unit building gets 1 STR max), a $100/unit annual fee and a mandatory off-street parking space per unit. That is a real upgrade in corroboration. But Municode's Warren code page returns only the site shell with no ordinance text, so the primary text remains unfetched for a third run. Honest status: high confidence, not confirmed. Still needs Warren Town Hall. Directly constrains both Warren RI duplexes below, since under a 50% rule a two-unit building can only ever run one STR side. EastBayRI, post-vote · EastBayRI, pre-vote.
  • North Kingstown RI — re-confirmed: no adopted STR ordinance or registry. Officials were "mulling" a registry in late 2022/early 2023; nothing has been adopted since. This makes North Kingstown the least-encumbered market in the set. The Independent.
  • South Kingstown RI — re-confirmed two-track. A general STR ordinance, plus a separate ADU track where Rhode Island state law (not merely local rule) bars ADUs from being offered as short-term or transient rentals on hosting platforms. The state-level prohibition overrides any local flexibility, so an ADU is not an STR income path here.
  • Jamestown RI — STR framework is active and functioning: 126 licensed STRs with 6 more pending, $350/yr registration, building and fire-marshal inspection, re-inspection every 2–3 years. The constraint in Jamestown is inventory, not rules — see below.
  • Buttonwoods Campground, Warwick RI — unresolved for an EIGHTH consecutive run. One quick check per the standing instruction: the Campers Association's own site and a Warwick historical writeup are both reachable, but neither publishes membership or rental-transfer rules in fetchable text. This will not be solved by searching. It needs a phone call to the Association — recommend Bill either make that call or formally drop Buttonwoods from the watch list.
