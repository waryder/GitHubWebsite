Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — permit cap STILL ENJOINED, no final ruling found. The 2024 ordinance (caps of 1,100 in 2024 → 1,000 in 2025 → 900 by September 2026, a 7-night minimum stay, and event bans) remains blocked by the preliminary injunction Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted in October 2024 in Narragansett 2100 v. Town of Narragansett. The most recent documented town statement, a 31 January 2025 press release, put the resolution date at "unknown" and confirmed the town continues to register rentals only under the pre-existing ordinance (§§14-518–14-520). No 2026 trial date, settlement or appellate decision could be found this run — treat any claim that the cap is now in force as false until a dated 2026 source says otherwise. Sources: Patch, Boston Globe.
  • Rhode Island statewide — 5% whole-home STR tax RE-CONFIRMED in effect since 1 January 2026, on top of the existing 7% sales tax and the local hotel tax doubling from 1% to 2%. The tax keys off the date of occupancy, not the booking date. Sources: RI Division of Taxation ADV 2025-16, RI Realtors.
  • Warwick RI — NEW material restriction. The 2026 zoning update bars new non-owner-occupied short-term rentals in the R-6 and R-10 residential zones, which cover much of Oakland Beach and Gaspee. STRs also remain banned in commercial and industrial zones. Registration is required with both the state DBR ($25/yr) and the city, with annual building and fire inspections and a local contact able to respond within 30 minutes. This directly constrains the cheapest Section 2 candidates above — confirm the parcel's zone before any Warwick RI purchase intended for absentee weekly rental. Sources: City of Warwick, 2026 guide.
  • South Kingstown RI — residential rental registration ordinance adopted 14 October 2025 (short-term defined as 30 days or fewer). Registration required before any occupancy; $100 fee, $300 late fee, fines up to $500. The filing deadline was extended to 31 March 2026. Source: Town of South Kingstown.
  • Jamestown RI — active registration ordinance: register with the state DBR first, then a local OpenGov account; $350 per year; fire marshal inspection every two years and building inspection every three. The registration window opens each fall for the following year. Source: Jamestown Press.
  • North Kingstown RI — no dedicated townwide STR ordinance or registry found; the town manages through existing zoning, building and assessor rules. State DBR registration still applies. The council was weighing options as of 2023 with no resolution confirmed this run. Sources: The Independent, RI DBR.

Coverage & Caveats

  • Source gating was inconsistent across agents this run, which is itself worth recording. On the northern corridor sweep (Canton / Stoughton / Sharon / Norwood MA), Zillow and Redfin were fully JS-gated and every Zillow figure there came second-hand from search snippets; LandWatch's Canton MA city page returned a false "0 Listings" while Homes.com showed three real parcels — treat that zero as a false negative. On the southern sweep (Mansfield / Attleboro / Rehoboth MA), Zillow and Redfin returned full server-rendered HTML and were used directly, which is atypical and gave unusually deep visibility. On the RI sweep, Zillow and Redfin were gated again throughout.
  • Because gating differed by agent, coverage depth is not uniform across towns. Rehoboth MA and Attleboro MA are the best-covered towns in this report; Bonnet Shores RI, North Kingstown RI and Saunderstown RI are the thinnest. Rehoboth MA's depth advantage is partly real and partly an artifact of better source access.
  • 106 Indian Ln, Canton MA now has three mutually incompatible data points across three runs: $329,000 / 1.0 acre (LandSearch, prior run), $835,000 sale recorded 2026-03-17, and $1,499,999 / 1.16 acre (LandVest, this run, page itself JS-rendered and confirmable only through a search snippet). No two of these can both be right. This parcel needs direct confirmation with the listing broker before it is treated as real inventory in any direction.
  • Norwood Hospital remains closed. Shut since the June 2020 flood, still closed as of August 2026, with a June 2026 task force report pushing for state acquisition and two operators reportedly in talks with the site's owner — but no reopening date. All Norwood MA and Canton MA hospital estimates in this report route to Beth Israel Deaconess Milton or Needham instead.
  • Non-MLS channels — Craigslist, for-sale-by-owner, Facebook Marketplace, auction sites — were not swept this run; the standing criteria scope this report to MLS-aggregator sources.
  • Rental rate benchmarks are directional averages from AirDNA marketing pages and VRBO/Airbnb search summaries, not licensed exports. Planning-grade only.
  • All drive times are estimates from general road geography, not turn-by-turn routing.
  • Price discrepancies between Homes.com and Zillow appear on 18 North Ave and 38 Dean St and on several Attleboro MA three-family listings — most likely sync lag after recent price changes. Confirm any asking price with the listing agent before acting.
