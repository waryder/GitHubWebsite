STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI: the 2024 ordinance (permit caps, 7-night minimum, higher fees) remains enjoined by the Oct 2, 2024 Superior Court order in Narragansett 2100 v. Town (WC-2024-0372); no final judgment, appeal result or repeal found. The town registers rentals under the older ordinance ($120/unit/yr). No 2026 update could be confirmed — litigation still treated as unresolved. Court decision · summary
  • South Kingstown RI: registration ordinance adopted 4/8/2024; town said 11/13/2024 it would refrain from enforcing after the Narragansett ruling. 2026 enforcement unconfirmed. Town notice
  • North Kingstown RI: annual registration (~$100) + local representative; STR registration forfeits the Homestead Exemption (Dec 2023 table, may be dated).
  • Jamestown RI: registration $700/yr non-owner-occupied ($350 owner-occupied), local rep, inspection every 2 yrs, daily fines. RILCT analysis
  • Warwick RI: restricted — permits only in certain zones, $250/unit/yr, inspection, 2 persons/bedroom, $500/day fines; a reported plan would eliminate most of the city's STRs (outcome unverified). City page
  • Warren RI: capped — 125 STR units town-wide, max 50% of units in a multifamily, parking + registration (2023 article; may be revised). EastBayRI
  • Barrington RI: regulated through zoning with permit, local rep and occupancy limits.
  • Statewide: 5% RI whole-home STR tax on stays ≤30 days, effective 2026-01-01, plus local hotel tax rising 1%→2%. RI Realtors
