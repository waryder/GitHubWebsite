STR rules by town

  • Statewide (RI): 5% whole-home STR tax effective 2026-01-01 on stays of 30 days or fewer (house, condo or mobile home); platforms remit, direct-booking owners register with Taxation — RI Division of Taxation FAQ. State DBR STR registration also applies.
  • Narragansett RI: 2024 ordinance (permit cap, 7-night minimum, permit fees, $1M insurance) remains under the Oct 2, 2024 Superior Court preliminary injunction (Narragansett 2100 case); no final judgment found this run. STRs register under the existing rental ordinance ($120/unit/yr) — VRMA, court decision. Litigation still open.
  • South Kingstown RI: registration ordinance adopted 4/8/2024; Town said 11/13/2024 it would refrain from enforcing — current status unconfirmed — Town notice.
  • Jamestown RI: STR ordinance (amended 8/7/2023) with registration and inspection; caps unconfirmed — Town Clerk.
  • Warwick RI: STR permit required via OpenGov; press reported a plan that would eliminate most of the city's 73 STRs — enactment unconfirmed — City page, Providence Journal via Yahoo. This matters for all three Oakland Beach buys above.
  • North Kingstown RI: third-party guide says registration only, no STR-specific zoning limits (unverified) — STR Profit Map.
  • Excluded — community prohibits renting: Mary Carpenter's Beach Meadow, 854 Matunuck Beach Rd, South Kingstown RI; Roy Carpenter's Beach, 240 Cards Pond Rd, South Kingstown RI; and newly found 64-46 Burnside Ave (Knowles Camp), Narragansett RI, $259,999, 504 sf seasonal cabin — listing states "NO RENTALS" (Raveis).
