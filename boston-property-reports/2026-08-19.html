Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — 2024 permit-cap ordinance STILL ENJOINED, no 2026 merits ruling found. The May 2024 ordinance (permit cap starting at 1,100 and dropping to 900 by September 2026, plus a 7-night minimum stay) was hit with a TRO and preliminary injunction by Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter on 2024-10-02 in Narragansett 2100, Inc. v. Town of Narragansett (No. WC-2024-0372), the court finding a reasonable likelihood of success on preemption grounds. As of this run, law-firm tracking (Partridge Snow & Hahn, last updated May 2026) and a South County brokerage still describe the case as pending with enforcement paused — no trial on the merits and no RI Supreme Court decision found. Treat the ordinance as unenforceable but not repealed, and re-check before underwriting.
  • South Kingstown RI — annual Short-Term Residential Rental registration (adopted April 2024), $100 fee, $300 late fee, fines to $500. Registration deadline extended to 2026-03-31.
  • North Kingstown RI — no townwide STR registry ordinance found. Regulated informally via zoning, building and assessor review; homestead tax status can be affected by short-term leasing. Council has discussed tracking STRs as businesses but no ordinance is confirmed as of this run.
  • Jamestown RI — local registration required since August 2023: $350/year, building and fire-marshal inspection (fire re-inspection every 2 years, building every 3), plus state DBR registration.
  • Warwick RI — registration via city portal. The 2024 ordinance update requires guest ID and vehicle info retained 180 days, noise/nuisance compliance, smoke and CO detectors, no outdoor fires, and grills 10 ft from structures. Violations can suspend or revoke a permit.
  • Barrington RI — STR ordinance (May 2022, Zoning Art. XXXIII): registration with Barrington Police, a 24/7-reachable local contact, occupancy capped at 2 persons per bedroom.
  • Warren RI — ordinance caps total STR units townwide, requires annual registration and 1 off-street parking space per unit, and bars any visible exterior change from residential use.
  • Statewide baseline — R.I. Gen. Laws §42-63.1-14 bars towns from prohibiting owners from advertising on hosting platforms if the unit is registered with RI DBR and taxes are remitted, but does not bar zoning or special-use-permit requirements (confirmed by the January 2025 Hughes v. Town of Exeter decision upholding Exeter's special-use-permit rule). ADUs cannot legally be offered as STRs statewide (R.I. Gen. Laws §45-24-73).
  • Statewide RI tax — confirmed in effect. The 5% whole-home STR tax took effect 2026-01-01 for entire homes/condos rented 30 days or less, and the local hotel tax doubled from 1% to 2% the same day. Combined lodging rate for whole-home STR guests: up to 14% (7% state sales tax + 5% state hotel tax + 2% local hotel tax, with the whole-home 5% applied per the RI Division of Taxation occupancy-date rule so no single stay is charged both 5% levies). Sources: RI DOT Advisory 2025-16, RI DOT Notice 2025-09, Sales Tax Institute, RI Association of Realtors.

Norwood Hospital — status re-verified this run

  • Still closed. No ER. No reopening date. No operator named. Closed since the catastrophic flood of 2020-06-28; former operator Steward Health Care went bankrupt in 2024 and abandoned its rebuild.
  • Eminent domain is moving forward. Gov. Healey signed a bill (reported 2026-08-09) authorizing DCAMM to seize the 11-acre site from owner Medical Properties Trust, effective 2026-11-01. The Senate passed the measure 2026-07-29; the House enacted it 2026-07-31.
  • Rep. Stephen Lynch says two hospital operators remain in active talks with MPT, out of roughly six that floated proposals since 2024.
  • Mass General Brigham had explored buying the site, but MPT reportedly raised its ask from $250M to $375M and that deal appears stalled.
  • Run note on the signing date. Earlier runs in this series recorded the signing as 2026-08-04 and 2026-08-07; this run's best source (Boston.com, 2026-08-09) puts it at roughly 2026-08-09. The reporting is inconsistent across outlets — treat early August 2026 as the confirmed window rather than any single day.
  • Consequence for the corridor: the ≤15-minute hospital rule continues to fail at the Canton / Stoughton / Sharon / Norwood MA end, where the nearest open ER runs an estimated 15–25 min. It argues, as it has all month, for the Attleboro MA and Mansfield MA end near Sturdy Memorial.

Coverage & Caveats

  • Accessible sources this run: Homes.com (search grids and full property detail pages including MLS-level fields, unit counts, tax history and price history), Land.com, LandWatch, LandSearch, William Raveis, eXp Realty, and Boston Pads for MLS history. Redfin detail pages loaded when a direct URL was already known.
  • Gated or blocked: Zillow and Redfin search grids were largely JS-gated corridor-wide — usable for headline counts and the first one to three results but not paginable. LandWatch was reachable in the northern cluster only. jacobrealty.com returned a hard JS redirect wall and massneighborhoods.com returned empty content. AirDNA town pages could not be fetched directly (account/paywall), so every rate figure is a search-summary of those pages rather than the raw dashboard.
  • Estimated unconfirmed inventory by cluster: 15–20% for Canton/Stoughton/Sharon/Norwood MA · 10–15% for Attleboro/Mansfield MA and 15–20% for Rehoboth MA · 25–40% for the RI beach corridor, which was the most heavily gated. Concentrated in the newest listings and any agent-exclusive or pocket listings not yet syndicated.
  • All drive times are estimates based on town geography and known facility locations (Sturdy Memorial Hospital, 211 Park St, Attleboro MA; Attleboro Public Library, 74 N Main St; Blanding Public Library, 124 Bay State Rd, Rehoboth MA; Mansfield Public Library; Planet Fitness at 40 Cumberland Ave, North Attleborough MA and 175 Mansfield Ave, Norton MA). None were computed per parcel with a routing tool.
  • Utilities: most Rehoboth MA and rural Mansfield/Attleboro MA parcels are well-and-septic by default. Where a listing did not state water/sewer explicitly it is marked unknown rather than guessed — and no listing was dropped for unknown utilities.
  • RI rate benchmarks are directional, not audited. South Kingstown RI and Bonnet Shores RI have no town-level AirDNA data at all; those rows use nearby comps and individual listing prices as proxies.
  • Not independently verified: live-URL deploy verification of this page after publish was not performed.
