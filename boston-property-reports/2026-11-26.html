STR rules by town

  • Rhode Island statewide: 5% whole-home STR tax effective 2026-01-01 confirmed (applies to entire dwellings incl. condos and mobile homes, stays ≤30 days), on top of state sales/hotel taxes; DBR registration required. RI Division of Taxation FAQ
  • Narragansett RI: restricted / in litigation. 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum, permit cap 1,100 → 1,000 → 900, $1M liability, inspections) was enjoined by Superior Court in Oct 2024 (Narragansett 2100 v. Town); registration paused and the older $120/unit registration ordinance applies. 2026 status UNCONFIRMED this run — no 2026 primary news found; a GoLocalProv article headlined as a landlord victory could not be fetched. court decision · Patch 1/2025 · WhatsUpNewp 3/2025
  • South Kingstown RI: registry ordinance (4/2024, $100/yr), but the Town suspended enforcement and refunded 2025 fees in 11/2024; 2026 enforcement status not confirmed. Town notice
  • Jamestown RI: STR ordinance (amended 8/2023) with eligibility, inspections, Clerk registration; details not retrieved; water-use concerns raised. Town Clerk
  • Warwick RI: registration required via city portal; fees/restrictions not retrieved. City page
  • North Kingstown RI, Bonnet Shores (Narragansett RI): not researched this run.
