STR rules by town (as of 2026-08-13)

  • Statewide RI: mandatory DBR registration for any STR advertised on a hosting platform ($25/yr) — dbr.ri.gov. 5% whole-home STR tax in force since 2026-01-01, on top of existing hotel taxes (combined short-stay take can reach ~14%) — RI Div. of Taxation FAQ.
  • Narragansett RI — ordinance still enjoined/unenforced. The 2024 permit-cap ordinance (7-night minimum, cap stepping 1,100→900 by 2026) remains stayed under the October 2024 preliminary injunction (Narragansett 2100 suit, preemption argument under RI Gen. Laws §42-63.1-14). Most recent reporting found (Dec 2025) has the Town Council drafting a zoning-based rewrite; no 2026 ruling located this run — treat as "enjoined, actively being rewritten," not settled. Boston Globe, Narragansett Times, PS&H analysis (May 2026).
  • South Kingstown RI: STR registry since April 2024 — $100/yr due Dec 31, $300 late fee, posted-notice rule, fines to $500; town-wide incl. Matunuck. Town site.
  • North Kingstown RI: no dedicated townwide STR ordinance found; managed via zoning/building rules. State registration still mandatory.
  • Jamestown RI: registration since Aug 2023 — $350/yr, building + fire inspections, occupancy cap (2/bedroom + 2), parking minimums, 2-hour local-contact rule, revocable after 3 violations/12 mo. Guide.
  • Warwick RI: Feb 2023 ordinance — STRs by-right in residential/mixed-use village zones, prohibited in commercial/industrial; safety requirements, 180-day guest records, fine/revocation exposure. warwickri.gov.

  • Narragansett RI STR litigation status is current only through December 2025 reporting plus a May 2026 legal analysis; no August 2026 ruling was located — re-verify before any purchase decision there.
