STR rules by town

  • Statewide RI: from 2026-01-01 a 5% tax on whole-home STRs (30 days or less) and local hotel tax 1% to 2%; trafficking-awareness training required.
  • Narragansett RI: the 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum, permit cap, inspections) remains under a 10/02/2024 TRO/preliminary injunction (Narragansett 2100 Inc v. Town, WC-2024-0372). A rescind motion failed 2-2; a zoning re-enactment was proposed with no votes found. Final judgment/appeal outcome unconfirmed - re-check. Bonnet Shores is in Narragansett.
  • South Kingstown RI: registration ordinance adopted 04/08/2024, enforcement suspended (notice 11/13/2024).
  • North Kingstown RI: annual registration $100, registered agent; registering forfeits Homestead Exemption (2023 table, may be stale).
  • Jamestown RI: allowed with compliance certificate, clerk registration and council license; max 2 guests/bedroom; no ADUs or cesspool properties. A 2026 staff proposal would move rules into zoning and limit non-hosted rentals to four commercial districts (not adopted).
  • Warwick RI: STR by right only in A-40/A-15/A-10/A-7 and Village District; barred in Waterfront Business, General Business and others; $250/yr per unit plus inspection. Check each parcel's zoning.
  • Barrington RI: permit required, max 2 guests/bedroom. Warren RI: $100/unit by Dec 1; multifamily may use at most 50% of units as STRs.
