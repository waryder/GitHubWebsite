Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — still unresolved. The May 2024 ordinance set a declining permit cap (1,100 in 2024 → 1,000 in 2025 → 900 in 2026), a 7-night minimum stay, and an event/wedding ban. In October 2024 RI Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted a TRO and preliminary injunction in a Home Rule Amendment challenge brought by the landlord group Narragansett 2100, and enforcement has been paused ever since. The most recent confirmed reporting (Narragansett Times, 2025-12-19) has the Town Council reworking the ordinance to re-codify it as a zoning ordinance on the solicitor's advice, with no vote taken and no resolution date. Nothing newer than December 2025 could be found, so as of 2026-08-12 the cap appears to remain unenforced heading into the 2026 season and the operative rule is the town's older registration ordinance (Sec. 14-518–520, $120/unit). Treat Narragansett as regulatorily uncertain — a real risk factor, not paperwork. Sources: Boston Globe, WPRI, Narragansett Times, ordinance text.
  • Statewide RI 5% whole-home STR tax — confirmed in force since 2026-01-01. Applies to short-term rentals of an entire residential dwelling for 30 nights or fewer. The local hotel tax also doubled from 1% to 2% statewide on the same date. Both apply based on date of occupancy, not booking. Also effective 2026-01-01: STR operators must complete annual human-trafficking-awareness training, and ADUs remain statutorily barred from transient/STR use. Sources: RI Division of Taxation Notice 2025-09, Advisory 2025-16, RI Association of Realtors.
  • Jamestown RI — has an active, functioning ordinance, unlike Narragansett: 126 licensed STRs as of 2026, $350/yr fee, biennial fire and building inspection, occupancy capped at 2 per bedroom plus 2, an RI-resident local representative with a 2-hour response requirement, and 1 parking space per bedroom plus 1. The council reserves the right to cap total permits but has not done so. Jamestown Press
  • South Kingstown RI (incl. Wakefield and Matunuck) — annual STR registration required; $300 late fee; fines up to $500 for violations.
  • North Kingstown RI (incl. Wickford and Saunderstown) — no dedicated STR registry; regulated through zoning, building and assessor processes. STR use can affect homestead tax exemption status; confirm with Building/Zoning before purchase.
  • Warwick RI — no dedicated STR ordinance surfaced this run; contact town offices directly before committing.
  • Statewide baseline, every town: DBR registration is required before listing on any platform, and the registration number and expiration must appear on the listing (R.I. Gen. Laws § 42-63.1-14).
