Short-term rental rules by town

  • Statewide Rhode Island. DBR registration is mandatory before listing on any platform; the registration number and expiration must appear on the listing. ADUs may not be short-term rented. Effective 2026-01-01, a new 5% whole-home STR tax applies to entire-home stays of 30 nights or fewer, and the local hotel tax rose from 1% to 2% (no double tax on a single stay). Sources: Sales Tax Institute, Avalara, RI Division of Taxation FAQ.
  • Narragansett RI — re-checked this run, still unresolved. The 2024 ordinance would cap STR permits (1,100 initially, stepping down to 900 by September 2026), impose a 7-night minimum stay, and ban event use. In October 2024 Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted a TRO/preliminary injunction in Narragansett 2100 v. Town of Narragansett. Through the latest reporting located (~November 2025) the cap and permit rules remain paused pending litigation, which is still unresolved. No newer resolution was found this run. Sources: Boston Globe, WPRI, GoLocalProv.
  • South Kingstown RI (incl. Wakefield RI, Peace Dale RI, Matunuck RI). Annual STR registry adopted April 2024: $100 fee, $300 late fee, fines to $500. No cap, no ban. Source: Town of South Kingstown RI.
  • North Kingstown RI (incl. Wickford RI, Saunderstown RI). No dedicated townwide STR registry or ordinance could be confirmed. The council was "mulling" a registry as of January 2023; no adoption was confirmed this run. Zoning, building, and assessor rules apply case by case — confirm with Building/Zoning before purchase. Sources: The Independent, Steven Miller Group.
  • Warwick RI (incl. Oakland Beach, Conimicut, Buttonwoods, Warwick Neck). STR is a permitted use in residential and mixed-use village zones under the February 2023 ordinance as amended July 2024. Requires registration, annual inspection, and a local contact; fines to $500/day. No cap identified. Sources: TurnTo10, Warwick Advertiser.
  • Jamestown RI. $350/yr registration, building and fire inspections, occupancy cap (2 per bedroom + 2), one parking space per bedroom + 1, local representative must respond within 2 hours, permit revocable after 3 violations in 12 months. No cap on the number of permits. Source: BNBCalc regulation guide.
