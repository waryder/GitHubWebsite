Criteria in force this run
  • Section 1 — corridor land & homes, 0.75 to 5.0 acres inclusive. Vacant/buildable land, teardown-grade structures, or existing single-family homes. Public water and sewer preferred but never disqualifying. ≤10 min to an MBTA Providence/Stoughton line station; ≤15 min hospital; ≤30 min highly rated library; ≤30 min Planet Fitness. The manufactured-home requirement is dropped. Towns: Canton, Stoughton, Sharon, Norwood, Mansfield, Attleboro and Rehoboth MA — Rehoboth MA is included flagged, since its station drive times (~18–22 min est.) fail the rail rule.

  • Section 3 — two-unit dwelling duplexes with private entrances, across the combined Section 1 + Section 2 footprint. No acreage cap, no price cap, no minimum room count. Triple-deckers and three-families are excluded.
  • All drive times are estimates. Every address carries its two-letter state code.

The absolute ADR levels are usable as rough bands; the year-over-year percentages should be treated as artifacts and not carried into any pro forma.

Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — permit-cap ordinance still enjoined, unchanged this run. The 2024 ordinance (cap declining 1,100 → 900 permits by the 2026 permit year) remains under the preliminary injunction issued October 2024 by Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter in Narragansett 2100 v. Town of Narragansett, on a state-preemption argument. No reporting was found this run of the injunction being lifted or the case resolving, so as of September 2026 an owner should not assume the 900-permit cap governs the 2026–27 rental year. Boston Globe · WPRI · GoLocalProv
  • Rhode Island statewide 5% whole-home STR tax — confirmed in effect. Effective 1/1/2026, a 5% whole-home short-term rental tax applies, and the local hotel tax rose from 1% to 2%. Liability is determined by date of occupancy, not date of booking. RI Div. of Taxation ADV 2025-16 · NOTICE 2025-09 · What'sUpNewp
  • Narragansett RI "duplex STR ban" — remains resolved as a conflated premise. Re-confirmed with no change: the Town's own Q&A establishes that each unit of a legal two-family property receives its own STR permit. The "ban" narrative traced to an unrelated April 2024 new-construction proposal, not to existing duplexes. Nothing found this run contradicts that.
  • Warren RI — ordinance confirmed (via local reporting; Municode deliberately not fetched, since it returns a JavaScript shell). The ordinance caps total STR units town-wide, limits STRs to no more than 50% of the units in any single building, and requires a local responsible contact reachable within two hours. STRs predating the ordinance are grandfathered as legal non-conforming and are not subject to the 50% cap. EastBayRI · EastBayRI follow-up
  • Buttonwoods, Warwick RI — unresolved a thirteenth consecutive run. The governing Buttonwoods Campers Association publishes its rules only behind a members-only login at buttonwoodsbca.com. This is now definitively a phone call, not a search, and no further search effort should be spent on it.
