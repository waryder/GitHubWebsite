Short-Term Rental Rules by Town

  • Narragansett — contested. 2024 ordinance caps STR permits (1,100 → 900 by 2026) with registration and $375–$750 annual permit fees. Landlord group sued; Superior Court granted a preliminary injunction blocking enforcement in Oct 2024 and the litigation remains unresolved. Registration was still being collected into 2025 ($120/unit). Buy here expecting policy risk. Boston Globe · Town Q&A
  • South Kingstown — registration required, not banned. Rental Registry (Town Code §9-200–210), $100/yr, $300 late fee. No unit cap found. Town page
  • North Kingstown — no dedicated local ordinance found. Discussed but not adopted; relies on state DBR registration and zoning. Confirm current status with NK Building/Zoning before buying. The Independent
  • Jamestown — permitted but paperwork-heavy. $350/yr, building + fire inspections, local RI contact with 2-hr response, parking and occupancy caps; council may deny for cause and reserves the right to cap permits. Jamestown Press
  • Warwick — permitted by right in residential/mixed-use village zones, banned in commercial/industrial. City registration, 180-day guest log, safety equipment; fines to $500/day. No cap. City page
  • Statewide, effective Jan 1 2026: new 5% RI whole-home STR tax on entire homes/condos rented ≤30 days, plus local hotel tax up from 1% to 2%. Bake into any income model.

Coverage & Caveats

  • Zillow and Redfin are JavaScript-gated and were not scraped. Homes.com, LandSearch, LandWatch, Land.com, Compass, Coldwell Banker and RILiving were used instead — all draw from the same MLS PIN / RI MLS feeds, so overlap is high, but a handful of platform-exclusive or FSBO listings per town may be missed.
  • All drive times are estimates based on road geography — no live routing tool was used. Verify exact minutes before acting.
  • No corridor listing except 75 Fowler St (Mansfield) and 1234 Commerce Way (Attleboro) explicitly confirms public water AND public sewer. Several explicitly reference perc tests or septic designs, which implies no public sewer at that parcel.
  • Rehoboth's ~18–22 min station drive times fail the 10-minute rail criterion; it is included because it carries the deepest qualifying inventory and has been part of this search.
  • The named beach towns with the most established weekly-rental market (Narragansett, Matunuck, Bonnet Shores) run 20–30 min from Wickford Junction, not 15. North Kingstown/Wickford and the Warwick shore villages are the only areas that genuinely clear the 15-minute radius.
  • Rental-rate sources (AirDNA, Rabbu, Awning, Hichee) disagree with each other by wide margins. Treat all figures as directional benchmarks, not underwriting numbers.
  • Craigslist, FSBO, LandFlip and auction channels were checked and returned no confirmed matches; several are JS-heavy, so this is a soft gap rather than a confirmed zero.
