Coverage & caveats

  • The ~15-minute rail rule was WRONG and is corrected. Last run concluded that Wickford Junction is the line terminus and that Narragansett RI, Jamestown RI and Wakefield RI all sit 20–25 minutes beyond it, leaving the Warwick shore as the only passing geography. Actual measured drive times from Wickford Junction (1011 Ten Rod Rd, North Kingstown RI — confirmed still the terminus) are Narragansett RI ~16 min (11 mi), Jamestown RI ~16–17 min (11 mi), and Wakefield / South Kingstown RI ~24 min (11.7 mi). The correct statement of the rule is Warwick RI (easy pass) > Narragansett RI and Jamestown RI (borderline pass, ~16–17 min) > Wakefield RI (clear fail, ~24 min) — not the flat "only Warwick passes" framing carried previously. Rome2Rio · Waze
  • The ADR-versus-access tension sharpens rather than resolving. With the three gaps closed, Jamestown RI ($436) and Wakefield RI ($367) both beat Warwick RI ($263–264) by 40–65% — yet Jamestown RI is only a borderline rail pass and Wakefield RI clearly fails. Warwick RI is the sole clear rail pass and the weakest income market. No town in this dataset wins on both axes. The correction above does soften the picture: Narragansett RI at ~$538 ADR is now a borderline pass rather than a definite fail, which makes it the most interesting square on the board.
  • Norwood Hospital re-confirmed CLOSED, and the situation has not moved. The rebuild was abandoned in 2024 during Steward Health Care's bankruptcy; the site is an unfinished construction project owned by an Alabama-based REIT; a legislative eminent-domain bill has been filed; as of August 2026 two operators remain in talks — but there is no reopening date.
