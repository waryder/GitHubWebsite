  • Narragansett RI — 2024 ordinance: registration/permit, 7-night minimum, a declining permit cap (1,100 → 1,000 → 900 by Sept 2026), weddings and banquets banned. Under court injunction since Oct 2024 and not currently enforceable. Town Q&A · RICentral, Dec 2025
  • Bonnet Shores RI — falls under the Town of Narragansett RI ordinance above. No distinct fire-district STR rule could be confirmed either way.
  • North Kingstown RI and Saunderstown RI — no dedicated townwide STR ordinance found; general zoning plus state DBR registration. Town FAQ · RI DBR
  • South Kingstown RI / Wakefield RI — rental registry adopted 4/8/2024 (Town Code §§9-200–9-210), $100 annual fee, $300 late fee, registration deadline extended to 3/31/2026. Registration only — no cap, no ban. Town page
  • Jamestown RI — registration since Aug 2023, $350 annual fee, inspection required; the town reserves discretionary authority to cap by neighborhood or block but no fixed numeric cap was confirmed. Jamestown Press
  • Warwick RI — ordinance since Feb 2023 (O-23-4): STR permitted by right in A-40 / A-15 / A-10 / A-7 residential zones and the Village District; prohibited in commercial and industrial zones and in accessory dwelling units/structures; $250 city + $25 state fees. City memo to the RI STR Commission
  • Barrington RI — registration with the Police Department, 24/7 local contact required; ADUs explicitly barred from short-term/transient use; no townwide numeric cap found. Art. XXXIII · Art. XXII (ADU)
  • Warren RI — 2023 ordinance: townwide cap of 125 STR units, per-building cap of 50% of units (pre-existing grandfathered), $100/unit fee, registration required. No 2026 amendment, repeal or challenge was found — reported as "no evidence of change found," not as a confirmed "unchanged." EastBayRI direct fetches failed twice and were not retried a third time per the source-class rule; Municode was correctly never attempted. Direct confirmation with the Warren RI Town Clerk is the recommended next step.
  • Statewide RI — the FY2026 budget (H.5076 Sub. A, Art. 5 §6) imposes a 5% Whole Home Short-Term Rental Tax on entire-dwelling STRs effective 2026-01-01, on top of the 7% state sales tax and a 2% local hotel tax (raised from 1%) — roughly 14% combined exposure. Applies by occupancy date, not booking date. RI Division of Taxation FAQ · ADV 2025-16

The Narragansett RI "duplex STR ban" — RESOLVED as a conflated premise

This report carried a "Narragansett RI bans duplex short-term rentals" claim for weeks and flagged it as suspect last run. It is now resolved: there is no such ban, and duplexes can be short-term rented in Narragansett RI. The Town's own STR Q&A asks and answers the question directly — if there are two units on the same property, both need to be registered, each with a separate application and permit. The "ban" language traces to an entirely separate April 2024 zoning proposal to restrict new duplex construction in response to the state's 2023 housing law, covered by the Providence Journal on 4/17/2024 and reflected in Narragansett Planning Board minutes through April 2025. Two unrelated policy threads had been fused into one non-existent rule. Removing it materially improves the case for 17 Clara Ln, Narragansett RI, whose whole value rests on renting both halves.
