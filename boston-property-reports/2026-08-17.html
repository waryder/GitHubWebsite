Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — still enjoined, litigation unresolved. The 2024 ordinance requires registration ($120/unit), sets a declining permit cap (1,100 in 2024 → 1,000 in 2025 → 900 in 2026), imposes a 7-night minimum stay, and bans weddings and corporate events at STRs. The cap and minimum-stay provisions have been blocked since roughly October 2024 by a Superior Court preliminary injunction (Judge Sarah Taft-Carter) after Narragansett 2100 sued on home-rule grounds. Re-checked this run: no final ruling on the merits was found and no 2026-dated news confirming resolution surfaced — the injunction still appears to be in force and the cap unenforced. Sources: Boston Globe, WPRI.
  • South Kingstown RI. Registration required via the Building Official, $100 fee, $300 late fee; registration deadline extended to 2026-03-31; violations to $500. Under 30 days counts as short-term. No cap or ban found. Town page.
  • North Kingstown RI. No dedicated townwide STR registry found — handled through zoning, building and assessor processes, with state DBR registration still required. AirDNA flags the town as a high-regulation profile, so confirm permit availability by neighborhood before buying. RI DBR.
  • Warwick RI. Ordinance O-23-4 (2023): $250/unit/year permit, annual building and fire inspection, a local representative within 50 miles, and 180 days of guest recordkeeping. 31 days or fewer counts as short-term. Permitted by right only in A-7/A-10/A-15/A-40 and Village; prohibited in OS, Office, WB, GB, LI, GI, Intermodal and Gateway. No numeric citywide cap found. Town page.
  • Jamestown RI. Registration through the OpenGov portal, $350/year, Building Official and Fire Marshal inspections (re-inspection every 3 and 2 years respectively); registration opened January 2024. No hard cap found, though the town has discussed phased approval. Jamestown Press.
  • Statewide RI 5% whole-home STR tax — confirmed in effect. Since 2026-01-01 Rhode Island taxes whole-home short-term rentals (houses, condos, mobile homes let in their entirety for 30 days or fewer) at an additional 5%, and the local hotel tax rose from 1% to 2% the same day, bringing the combined lodging tax to 14% for both whole-home and room-only rentals. Taxability follows the date of occupancy, not the booking date. Sources: RI Division of Taxation FAQ, RI Association of Realtors.

Coverage & Caveats

  • Sources that worked: Homes.com (the workhorse across all three sections), LandSearch.com, Land.com, Compass.com, Raveis, and individual broker sites (H&K Realty, Mello Group). Town .gov ordinance pages and PDFs carried the Warwick RI and South Kingstown RI regulatory findings. AirDNA market-overview pages supplied the rate benchmarks.
  • Sources that blocked: Zillow and Redfin were JS-gated in every town and returned no usable listing grids; LandWatch's land-search views also render client-side and showed zero results even where inventory demonstrably exists, so they were treated as unreliable and cross-checked elsewhere. Two supporting pages returned empty content on fetch: the South Kingstown RI Cottage Community Regulations page and a secondary Warwick RI zoning blog. Estimated 10–20% of live inventory across the search area remains unconfirmed as a result — concentrated in Zillow/Redfin-exclusive and MLS-only listings.
  • Utilities are the weakest data point in Section 1. Public water and sewer availability was explicitly confirmed for essentially no in-band parcel in the north cluster; every such cell reads "unconfirmed" rather than a guess. Rehoboth MA is well and septic by default. Parcel-level confirmation still requires a call to the relevant town DPW.
  • All drive times are estimates derived from town geography and published distances, not live routing.
  • Rate benchmarks are directional — back-calculated from AirDNA trailing-twelve-month ADRs, not weekly-rate products. Treat them as a sanity check on the income side, not as underwriting input.
  • Stale-listing risk. Several addresses that surfaced through AI search summaries proved already sold on verification and were dropped: 3 Willow Way, 37 Davis St and 51 Fairview Ave, all Rehoboth MA, and 73-75 Draper Ave, Mansfield MA. One Rehoboth MA parcel remains in the table with a stale flag: the vacant-land listing at 52 Round Farm Rd, Rehoboth MA is ~14 months old and may be superseded by the built parcel at the same address.
