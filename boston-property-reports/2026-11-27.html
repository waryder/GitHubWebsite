STR rules by town

  • Rhode Island statewide: 5% whole-home STR tax on stays ≤30 days effective 2026-01-01 confirmed; local hotel tax on STRs rises 1% → 2%. RI Realtors
  • Narragansett RI: 2024 ordinance (permit cap 1,100 falling to 900, 7-night minimum) remains under the Oct 2, 2024 Superior Court preliminary injunction; secondary sources dated Sept 10, 2026 and Nov 15, 2026 say litigation is still pending. No 2025/2026 merits or appellate ruling found; docket not checked. Annual rental registration still applies. Justia · Phipps Realty · STR HUB
  • Warwick RI: permit $250/unit/yr; 2 persons per bedroom; STRs limited to specific zoning districts.
  • Warren RI: permit $100/unit/yr; multifamily may use at most 50% of units as STRs (affects duplexes).
  • North Kingstown RI: registration $100/yr, registered agent. Jamestown RI: permit $700/yr non-owner-occupied, $350 owner-occupied, inspection every 2 years. Barrington RI: license, 2 per bedroom. South Kingstown RI: no STR ordinance as of 12/2023; current status unverified.
  • Town table source is the RI League of Cities & Towns analysis dated 12/6/2023 — PDF; re-verify before buying.
