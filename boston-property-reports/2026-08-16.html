STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI: the 2024 permit-cap ordinance (cap stepping to ~900 by Sept 2026, 7-night minimum) remains under Superior Court preliminary injunction and unenforced; no 2025/2026 ruling, trial date or settlement found this run — litigation unresolved (Globe, WPRI).
  • South Kingstown RI: mandatory rental registry since April 2024 — $300/unit for STR ≤30 days (capped $1,000/owner), $300 late fee (town).
  • North Kingstown RI: no finalized town-wide STR ordinance found — town has debated options since 2023; confirm current status before purchase (Independent RI).
  • Jamestown RI: active registration regime — $125 base license + per-bedroom fees ($50–$200/bd by residency, capped at 4 bd), fire-marshal inspections, RI-resident contact required; council reserves right to cap total STRs (Jamestown Press).
  • Warwick RI: STRs permitted in residential/mixed-use village zones under the Feb 2023 ordinance (safety equipment, guest registry); possible further tightening under discussion (Warwick Beacon).
  • Bonnet Shores RI: governed by the (enjoined) Narragansett RI ordinance; no separate neighborhood rule found.
  • Barrington RI / Warren RI: no STR ordinance details surfaced this run — unconfirmed.
  • Statewide RI: the 5% whole-home STR tax is in force since 2026-01-01; combined lodging tax on whole-home STRs now totals 14% (local hotel tax raised 1%→2%) (RI Div. of Taxation).
