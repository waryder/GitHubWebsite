Short-term-rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — rechecked this run. The 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum stay; permit cap of 1,100 from Sept 2024 dropping to 900 from Sept 2026) remains frozen under the preliminary injunction issued by Judge Sarah Taft-Carter in the Narragansett 2100 landlord-group suit. The most recent reporting located is November 2025; no 2026 ruling was found and the case has not reached a merits decision, so the ordinance is still not being enforced. Treat Narragansett as unsettled — the town could prevail and reinstate the cap and the 7-night minimum at any time, which is real regulatory risk on any Narragansett purchase. Sources: Boston Globe, WPRI, Town of Narragansett.
  • Statewide RI 5% whole-home STR tax — confirmed in force. Effective 2026-01-01, Rhode Island imposes a 5% state tax on short-term rentals of entire homes/condos (30 days or less), on top of an increased local hotel tax (1% → 2%). Confirmed still in effect as of this run. Newport has a carve-out to collect both directly. Sources: RI Div. of Taxation FAQ, Notice 2025-09.
  • Statewide baseline. Every RI host must register with the Dept. of Business Regulation before listing on any platform, and the registration number and expiration must appear on the listing. ADUs may not be offered as STRs. Source: RI DBR.
  • South Kingstown RI. Annual short-term residential rental registration required (adopted 2024-04-08; rental-registration ordinance 2025-10-14). $100 fee, $300 late fee, fines to $500. Filing deadline extended to 2026-03-31. No ban or cap found. Source: Town of South Kingstown.
  • North Kingstown RI (incl. Saunderstown, Wickford). No dedicated townwide STR registry page found; regulated through existing zoning/building/assessor processes, and renting can affect homestead tax exemption status. Roughly 54 units on the state DBR registry at the last data point. Confirm directly with NK Building/Zoning before buying.
  • Jamestown RI. Town clerk registration, $350/yr, building-official and fire-marshal inspection, occupancy capped at 2 per bedroom + 2, and an RI-resident local contact who can respond within 2 hours. No ban identified.
  • Warwick RI — status unresolved. The February 2023 ordinance permits STRs in residential and mixed-use village zones (prohibited in commercial/industrial) with no owner-occupancy requirement. A separate proposal from Councilman Ladouceur to ban non-owner-occupied STRs was reported, but whether it passed, failed, or is still pending could not be confirmed this run. Verify the current ordinance text with Warwick's Building Department before buying. Sources: WarwickPost, City of Warwick.
  • Legal backdrop. A 2025 Superior Court decision, Hughes v. Town of Exeter, upheld a town's ability to require a special-use zoning permit for STRs even though state law protects the right to advertise on hosting platforms — towns retain real leverage through land-use law, not just registries. This is the same logic underlying the unresolved Narragansett case. Source: Justia.

Explicit exclusions — cottages that look perfect and cannot be rented

  • Mary Carpenter's Beach / Beach Meadow — 854 Matunuck Beach Rd, South Kingstown RI. Leased-land cottage community (Carpenter's Beach Meadow, Inc.) directly across from Matunuck's beach. The community's own for-sale page states, in the sellers' own listing text, that cottages at Site 23 ($170,000) and Site 30 ($235,000) are "On leased land, cannot be rented." Site 82 ($239,000) and Site 96 ($125,000, a 2018 trailer) are on the same leased land and presumed subject to the same rule. Buyers get seasonal personal use only (first Friday in May through October 15) with no subletting — a hard exclusion for Bill's strategy. Source: carpentersbeachmeadowinc.com.
  • Roy Carpenter's Beach — 240 Cards Pond Rd, South Kingstown RI. 377 leased-land summer cottages on a private beach; renting is not allowed in the community, by long-standing rule that preserves its family-only character, with cottages typically passing down within families. On 2025-10-29 the Roy Carpenter's Beach Association bought the underlying land for $16.3M, converting from a landlord-lease model to member/cooperative ownership — a major structural change, but the no-rental prohibition long predates it and there is no indication it was lifted. Assume it still applies and confirm with the Association in writing. Sources: WPRI, roycarpenters.com.
  • Others checked, no prohibition found: comparable leased-land colonies at East Matunuck RI, Snug Harbor RI and Charlestown RI showed no documented no-rental rule, and active VRBO/VacationCottage listings in those exact areas are themselves evidence there is no blanket ban. Even so, any visibly leased-land community — shared "Association, Inc." ownership, seasonal-only operation, site fees — should be treated as suspect until the bylaws are confirmed in writing. Mary Carpenter's and Roy Carpenter's establish this is a recurring pattern in the Matunuck / South Kingstown RI cottage market.
