STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — 2024 permit-cap ordinance (caps 1,100/1,000/900; 7-night minimum) enjoined in full by TRO/preliminary injunction 10/2/2024 in Narragansett 2100 v. Town, WC-2024-0372; court found the 7-night minimum likely preempted by G.L. 42-63.1-14 (decision). No 2025/2026 merits ruling found; a 2026 summary still describes enforcement as suspended (STR Profit Map). Litigation still unresolved as far as this run could find.
  • South Kingstown RI — 2024 registration ordinance (stays under 30 days); town paused enforcement and refunded 2025 fees after the Narragansett injunction (notice); current status unconfirmed.
  • Jamestown RI — town license by Oct 1, $125 + $30 fire inspection + $50–200/bedroom ($200 off-island owners), local rep within 2 hours, no cesspool properties or ADUs; proposal to confine non-hosted STRs to commercial districts not adopted as of July 2026 (BnbCalc, town page).
  • Warwick RI — by right in A-40/A-15/A-10/A-7 and Village District; prohibited in Waterfront Business and other commercial zones — check parcel zoning; $250/yr permit, local rep within 50 miles (BnbCalc).
  • Warren RI — town-wide cap of 125 STR units, max 50% of units in a building (matters for a duplex owner-occupant), $100/yr, local rep in Bristol County (EastBayRI).
  • North Kingstown, Barrington RI — not verified this run.
  • Statewide — 5% RI whole-home STR tax effective 2026-01-01, plus 7% sales and 2% local hotel tax (~14% combined); human-trafficking training required (RI Realtors, RI Taxation advisory).
