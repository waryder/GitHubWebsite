STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — 2024 permit-cap ordinance (7-night minimum; caps 1,100/1,000/900) enjoined 10/2/2024 in Narragansett 2100 v. Town (WC-2024-0372) (decision); rescission vote failed 2–2 (Independent). A third-party 8/2026 post says registration moved to the rental ordinance (§§14-518–520, $120/unit, due Aug 31) with no final judgment (Air Hostd). Current litigation status unconfirmed — no 2026 ruling found.
  • South Kingstown RI — 2024 registration ordinance; town paused enforcement and refunded 2025 registrations (notice); later repeal/amendment reported but unread — unverified.
  • Jamestown RI — licensed regime (town license, fire inspection, per-bedroom fee, local rep); cesspool properties and ADUs barred; pending staff proposal to limit non-hosted STRs to commercial districts — high risk for a non-resident buyer (bnbcalc).
  • Warwick RI — by right in A-40/A-15/A-10/A-7 and Village District; prohibited in Waterfront Business and other commercial districts — check parcel zoning on the shore; $250/yr permit (bnbcalc).
  • North Kingstown, Barrington, Warren RI — not verified this run.
  • Statewide — 5% RI whole-home STR tax effective 2026-01-01, plus 7% sales and 2% local hotel tax (~14% combined); $25 DBR registration (RI Realtors).
