STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — permit cap: the 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum; permit cap declining 1,100 in 2024 to 900 in 2026) remains under the preliminary injunction issued by Superior Court Associate Justice Sarah Taft-Carter in October 2024. No final ruling on the merits was found this run. Litigation from the "Narragansett 2100" landlord group continues, and Town Solicitor James Callaghan has discussed re-enacting the rules as a zoning ordinance rather than a standalone licensing ordinance. Bottom line: still not being enforced. Boston Globe · WPRI · Independent RI
  • Narragansett RI — duplex ban: DOWNGRADED this run from "being proposed" to "proposed, no action taken, still contested." The proposal is real — officials moved to ban duplexes townwide except by special permit in two zones, reported by the Providence Journal on 2024-04-17 as a response to the state's 2023 Shekarchi housing package. But the Town Council took no action, and the Planning Board separately voted 4–1 against the related sweeping zoning restructuring in April 2025 over safety, infrastructure and character concerns. No evidence found that a duplex ban has been adopted. Still a live constraint to watch on Section 3 there, but not a pending prohibition. Providence Journal (PressReader) · Independent RI
  • Warwick RI — R-6/R-10 bar: confirmed directionally for a second run — STRs are permitted in Warwick RI's residential and Village mixed-use zones generally but prohibited in the R-6 and R-10 districts, which cover much of the single-family shore stock. New detail this run: an earlier proposed owner-occupancy requirement was dropped by amendment (Councilman Anthony Sinapi). Primary sources again unreachable — Municode's Warwick RI zoning node returned a JS shell with no ordinance text, so all R-6/R-10 detail remains secondary-source only. Warwick Beacon · Policy Commons ordinance mirror · City of Warwick STR memo (2023 PDF)
  • Rhode Island statewide taxes — PRIMARY SOURCE REACHED this run: per the RI Division of Taxation's own notice, effective 2026-01-01 the local hotel tax rose from 1% to 2% on all short-term lodging, and a new 5% whole-home STR tax applies to entire-dwelling rentals of 30 days or less — separate from, and not stacked with, the existing 5% state hotel tax on single-room rentals. Full load on a taxable whole-home stay: 7% sales tax + 5% whole-home STR tax + 2% local hotel tax. RI Division of Taxation Notice 2025-09 (PDF)
  • South Kingstown RI — rental registry: re-confirmed. Adopted 2025-10-14; the original 2025-12-31 filing deadline was extended to 2026-03-31. $100 registration fee, $300 late fee. Applies to all rental dwellings including under-30-day stays. Town of South Kingstown RI (the CivicAlerts detail page returned an empty body on fetch — confirmed via town-site and local-press search snippets rather than a direct primary fetch)
  • North Kingstown RI, Jamestown RI, Bonnet Shores RI: not independently re-verified beyond general searches this run. No town-wide STR ban surfaced for North Kingstown RI. Confirm directly with each town's zoning office before any purchase.
