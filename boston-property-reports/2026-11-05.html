STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI: the 2024 ordinance (7-night minimum, permit cap, inspections) remains under the 10/02/2024 TRO/preliminary injunction (Narragansett 2100 Inc v. Town, WC-2024-0372; court found Zoning Enabling Act procedures were not followed and the 7-night minimum conflicts with G.L. 42-63.1-14). AirROI (checked 10/17/2026) says it is still on hold and rentals fall under general rental registration ($120/unit/yr). No final merits ruling or repeal found, but this was not confirmed against a primary docket - re-check. Bonnet Shores is in Narragansett.
  • Statewide RI: from 2026-01-01 a 5% tax on whole-home STRs (30 days or less) and local hotel tax raised from 1% to 2%; DBR registration $25/yr; fines $250/$500/$1,000; ADUs cannot be STRs; trafficking-awareness training required; towns cannot bar platform listings (AirROI, RI Realtors).
  • South Kingstown RI: rental registration ordinance adopted 10/14/2025, $300/unit/yr for rentals of 30 days or less, first deadline extended to 3/31/2026, not transferable; fines up to $500 per violation (AirROI, Steven Miller Group). Replaces the 2024 note that enforcement was suspended.
  • North Kingstown RI: no dedicated STR registry; relies on zoning/building/assessor rules, and short-term leasing can affect the homestead exemption (Steven Miller Group). Yesterday's $100/yr registration note is from a 2023 table and may be stale.
  • Jamestown, Warwick, Barrington, Warren RI: not re-confirmed this run. Carried from prior runs, unverified today: Jamestown requires a compliance certificate, clerk registration and council license; Warwick allows STR by right only in A-40/A-15/A-10/A-7 and Village District, $250/yr per unit; Barrington permit required; Warren $100/unit.
