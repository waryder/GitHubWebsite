STR Rules by Town

  • Narragansett RI — permit cap still ENJOINED and unenforceable. The 2024 ordinance would cap STR permits (1,100 → 1,000 → 900 by 2026) with a 7-night minimum and $375–750 annual permit fees. RI Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted a TRO and preliminary injunction in Sept–Oct 2024 for the "Narragansett 2100" landlord group on a home-rule-amendment challenge. As of the most recent reporting located (December 2025) the ordinance has been non-enforceable for over a year with litigation unresolved; no final ruling found as of today. Only the pre-existing rental registration is being enforced. Boston Globe · GoLocalProv · WPRI · Town Q&A
  • South Kingstown RI (incl. Wakefield, Peace Dale, Matunuck) — Short-Term Residential Rental Registry adopted April 2024 (Town Code Ch. 9, §§9-200–9-210). $100 annual registration, $300 late fee, posted-notice requirements. No numeric permit cap. Town of South Kingstown
  • Jamestown RI — RI DBR registration first, then a $350/yr town permit, biennial fire-marshal and building inspections, occupancy caps (2 per bedroom + 2), 1 parking space per bedroom + 1, local-contact-within-2-hours rule, three-strikes revocation. 126 STRs licensed for 2026; apply by Oct 1. No hard numeric cap. Jamestown Press
  • North Kingstown RI (incl. Wickford, Saunderstown) — no comprehensive townwide STR ordinance found. STRs are handled through existing zoning, building and assessor rules case-by-case; state DBR registration still required for any platform listing. Lower regulatory certainty — verify with NK Building/Zoning before purchase. The Independent · RI DBR
  • Warwick RI (incl. Oakland Beach, Conimicut, Buttonwoods, Warwick Neck) — Feb 2023 ordinance permits STR use in residential and mixed-use village zones, prohibits it in commercial/industrial. Town building-department permit required after RI DBR registration, plus safety-equipment requirements and 180-day guest-record retention. No numeric cap. WarwickPost · City of Warwick
  • Statewide RI 5% whole-home STR tax — CONFIRMED IN EFFECT. Took effect 2026-01-01 on whole-dwelling short-term rentals of 30 days or less, layered on the existing 5% state hotel tax framework (a single stay is never subject to both), plus the local hotel tax doubling from 1% to 2%. RI Division of Taxation presentation · RI Taxation FAQ · Sales Tax Institute · Avalara MyLodgeTax

Duplex zoning notes (verify with each town before an offer)

  • Barrington RI — predominantly single-family zoned (R-10, R-25, R-40). Existing two-families are generally legal non-conforming uses tied to specific older lots; new two-family construction is tightly restricted. The confirmed listings above sit in R-10 or NB (neighborhood business) zones.
  • Warren RI — denser historic village grid with legacy two- and three-family stock common in the Historic District and downtown core; comparatively more permissive of existing multi-family than its neighbors. This is why Warren RI carries four of the eleven confirmed RI duplexes.
  • Narragansett RI / South Kingstown RI — coastal zoning is largely single-family and low density; the two-family properties found are older non-conforming structures. Any redevelopment (notably the Moonstone Beach teardown) would need CRMC and coastal zoning review.
  • Jamestown RI / North Kingstown RI — overwhelmingly single-family zoned town-wide, entirely consistent with the zero inventory found. Conversion or new-build duplexes face steep zoning hurdles in both.
  • Warwick RI — zoning varies sharply by neighborhood. Central Warwick RI, where the confirmed duplex inventory concentrates, has more multi-family-permissive districts than the shore villages, which are largely single-family.
  • Canton MA — 26 Howard St, Canton MA is in GR (General Residence), confirmed multi-family zoning.
