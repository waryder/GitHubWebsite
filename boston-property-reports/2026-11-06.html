STR rules by town

  • Statewide RI: $25 DBR registration, renewed annually. 5% state tax on whole-home short-term rentals effective 2026-01-01 (guest pays; stays over 30 consecutive days exempt). RI Tax FAQ
  • Narragansett RI (restricted, litigation unresolved): May 2024 ordinance has a 7-night minimum, 900-permit cap from September 2026 and a 3-unrelated-occupant limit. Superior Court TRO and preliminary injunction issued Oct 2, 2024 (Narragansett 2100 Inc. v. Town of Narragansett, Superior-WC-2024-0372). As of an Aug 24, 2026 vendor blog enforcement was still enjoined; no final judgment or appeal result found. The Council vote to rescind failed 2-2. Town registers rentals under the older ordinance ($120/unit/yr). Decision Vendor summary
  • South Kingstown RI: registration ordinance adopted April 8, 2024 (stays under 30 days); enforcement stopped after the Oct 2024 injunction and fees were refunded; status since Nov 2024 unverified. Town notice
  • Jamestown RI (allowed, licensed): town license plus state registration; $125 plus $30 fire inspection plus per-bedroom fee; 2 persons per bedroom; local representative required. Staff proposed moving STRs into zoning and limiting non-hosted rentals to 4 commercial districts (not adopted as of the July 2026 packet): risk to non-hosted use. Vendor summary, not the Town's page. Source
  • North Kingstown RI: rentals under 30 days must be registered; owner-occupied R1-R3 allowed; whole-unit rental needs an annual temporary use permit; some zones may need a special use permit. Vendor page, not verified against town code. Source
  • Warwick RI: proposed ordinance PCO-4-22 sub A would allow STRs by right in A-40, A-15, A-10, A-7 with a $250/yr fee, annual inspections, 2 persons per bedroom; adoption status unknown. PCO-4-22
  • Bonnet Shores RI: falls under Narragansett rules; the beach association's covenants must be checked before buying.
  • Condos: rental policy not stated on any detail page except 72 W Shore Rd #202, Warwick RI; request condo documents before offering.
