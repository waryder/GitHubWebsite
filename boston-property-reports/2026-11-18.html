STR Rules

  • Statewide: 5% whole-home STR tax effective 2026-01-01 (stays >30 days and room rentals exempt; platforms remit) — RI Division of Taxation FAQ. State registration ~$25/yr plus local registration; usual 7% sales + 1% local tax also apply.
  • Narragansett RI: 2024 permit-cap ordinance (7-night minimum; cap stepping to 900 permits) remains under the 10/2/2024 Superior Court injunction (WC-2024-0372); an 8/24/2026 property-manager source says the case is not final. No later ruling found — not confirmed from an official source. Annual registration under the older rental ordinance still applies ($120/unit, due Aug 31).
  • Zoning: RI towns may regulate STRs via zoning/special-use permits (upheld in Exeter and Newport cases).
  • North Kingstown RI, South Kingstown RI, Jamestown RI, Warwick RI, Barrington RI, Warren RI: not re-checked this run — treat as unknown.
