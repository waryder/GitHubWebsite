Short-Term Rental Rules by Town

  • Narragansett RI — permit-cap ordinance still enjoined and unenforced. The May 2024 ordinance (caps 1,100→900 permits by 2026, 7-night minimum) was preliminarily enjoined by Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter on 2024-10-02 after a suit by the “Narragansett 2100” landlord group. A December 2024 council vote to rescind failed 2–2, so the ordinance remains on the books but unenforceable. As of the most recent dated sources (Oct–Dec 2025) the town solicitor recommended rewriting the rules as a proper zoning ordinance; no vote was taken. No 2026-dated source confirms a verdict, settlement, repeal or reinstatement — this remains genuinely unresolved. Landlords currently register under the older ordinance (§14-518–520, $120/unit). Verify with Narragansett Building Inspection (401-782-0613) before assuming either outcome. Boston Globe · GoLocalProv · Narragansett Times
  • Statewide RI — 5% whole-home STR tax confirmed in effect. Enacted in the FY2026 budget, effective 2026-01-01 based on occupancy date, on gross receipts for whole-home rentals of 30 days or less. It stacks on the existing 7% state sales tax and the local hotel tax (raised to 2% on 2026-01-01), for a total of roughly 14% on gross receipts. Filed via Form RI-8478/RI-STR; Airbnb and VRBO collect and remit on the host's behalf. No repeal found as of August 2026. RI Division of Taxation FAQ · RI Association of Realtors
  • Warwick RI — most restrictive zoning of the target towns. STR Operating License required since Feb 2023: $150 base plus $100/bedroom, capped at $500/yr, with $500K+ liability insurance and a local contact reachable within 30 minutes. STRs are banned in commercial and industrial zones, in ADUs, in accessory structures, and in affordable units. Historical enforcement has been loose (only 13 of 20+ listings licensed as of 2023), but that is not a plan. Every Warwick candidate above needs its zoning district checked before purchase.
  • South Kingstown RI — registry since April 2024, expanded October 2025 to cover all rentals. $300/unit for stays of 30 days or less; registration deadline extended to 2026-03-31. No minimum stay and no cap found. The least restrictive of the regulated towns.
  • Jamestown RI — state DBR registration plus local OpenGov registration, annual deadline October 1. $125 base plus $50–200/bedroom, biennial fire inspection, 126 licensed STRs in 2026. New for 2026: mandatory human-trafficking awareness training for operators. Council reserves the right to cap but has not done so.
  • North Kingstown RI — no dedicated STR ordinance found; general zoning, building code and state DBR rules apply. Coverage here is thin — verify directly with the Planning and Building departments.
  • Bonnet Shores (Narragansett RI) — residential homes fall under Narragansett's currently-unenforced ordinance. The separate Bonnet Shores Beach Club cabana association regulates whole-season leasing only, not nightly or weekly rentals. No distinct residential STR rule found.
