Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — permit-cap ordinance still frozen under injunction. The May 2024 ordinance (Chapter 1123) imposed a 7-night minimum stay, a permit cap declining from 1,100 in 2024 to 1,000 in 2025 and 900 in 2026, resident/non-resident fee tiers of $425/$850, and bans on weddings and parties. In Narragansett 2100, Inc. v. Town of Narragansett, Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted a TRO and preliminary injunction around September–October 2024 halting enforcement of the cap and other key provisions. The most recent dated source found is 30 October 2025, which confirms the ordinance was still enjoined and the litigation unresolved; no 2026-dated ruling, settlement or dismissal was found. The town continues to operate under its pre-existing general rental-registration rules ($120/unit, annual renewal, inspection, "three-student" rule). Verify directly with the Building Inspection Department (401-782-0613) before relying on this. Sources: Boston Globe, WPRI, The Offer Sheet (2025-10-30), ordinance text.
  • Statewide RI — 5% whole-home STR tax, effective 2026-01-01, confirmed in force. Applies to stays of 30 nights or less where the entire dwelling is rented; distinct from the 7% state sales tax, and no stay is double-taxed by both the hotel tax and the whole-home tax. The local hotel tax also rose from 1% to 2% on the same date. Combined burden on a whole-home weekly rental: 7% + 5% + 2% = 14% of gross receipts. Tax is keyed to date of occupancy, not date of booking. Sources: RI Division of Taxation notice, RI DOT whole-home FAQ, RI Association of Realtors.
  • South Kingstown RI — Short-Term Residential Rental Registry adopted 8 April 2024 (Town Code Ch. 9 §§9-200–9-210). Any rental under 30 days must register with the Building Official before occupancy. $300/unit, $300 late fee if not filed by Dec 31 annually; lease and registration must be posted at the primary entrance. No permit cap and no minimum-stay rule — materially less restrictive than Narragansett RI. Town registration page.
  • Jamestown RI — register with RI DBR first, then apply locally via OpenGov by October 1 annually for the following year (except on ownership transfer). $350/yr, inspection by building official and fire marshal, $1M liability insurance strongly recommended, and a required local contact who is an RI resident able to respond within 4 hours. Reduced fee available with proof of 183+ days/year residency. Statewide as of 2026-01-01, STR operators must also complete annual human-trafficking-awareness training. Jamestown Press, RI DBR.
  • Warwick RI — annual registration with both the RI DBR and the city is required before any guest occupies the unit. Annual inspections; occupancy capped at 2 people per bedroom; guest names, addresses and vehicle info must be retained for at least 180 days. Violations up to $500/day, and two or more violations can trigger permit revocation. Apply via the city's OpenGov STR portal through the Building Department. Warwick Building Dept.
  • North Kingstown RI — no dedicated town-wide STR registry or ordinance found this run. The town manages rental use through general zoning, building and assessor rules, and short-term leasing can affect homestead tax exemption status. State DBR registration still applies to anything listed on a third-party platform. Confirm permitted use with Building/Zoning and the Assessor before buying here for STR — the absence of an ordinance is not the same as permission. Municode.
