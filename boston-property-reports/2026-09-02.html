STR rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — permit cap: still enjoined, litigation unresolved. Cap structure 1,100 permits (2024) → 1,000 (2025) → 900 (2026) with a 7-night minimum. Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted a preliminary injunction in October 2024; a December 2024 council vote to rescind failed 2–2, leaving the ordinance on the books but unenforced. Boston Globe · Town Q&A · The Independent
  • Narragansett RI — duplex “ban”: still unadopted. A May 2025 package of 14 zoning amendments that would have loosened two-family/duplex restrictions was withdrawn after resident opposition and restarted via workshops; no restrictive duplex ordinance has been adopted either. The pre-existing restrictive zoning simply persists unchanged. The Independent · WPRI
  • Statewide RI — 5% whole-home STR tax CONFIRMED from primary source. The RI Division of Taxation's December 2025 FAQ confirms the 5% Whole Home Short-Term Rental Tax effective 2026-01-01, applying to entire houses, condos and mobile homes rented 30 days or fewer, stacking with the (now 2%) local hotel tax and the 7% sales tax. Occupancy date governs the rate, not booking date. RI Division of Taxation FAQ (PDF)
  • South Kingstown RI — two distinct ordinances, kept separate. (a) STR registry adopted 2024-04-08, $100 fee / $300 late fee, rentals under 30 days only (town notice). (b) All-rentals registry adopted 2025-10-14, covering every rental dwelling — beachside weekly cottages and year-round apartments alike — deadline extended from 2025-12-31 to 2026-03-31 (town page). Search summaries routinely blend these into one; they are separate.
  • Warwick RI — R-6/R-10 bar: now actively CONTRADICTED by the city's own primary source. Escalating from three runs of “secondary-source-only”: the City of Warwick's own October 2023 memo to the state STR legislative commission was fetched directly this run and describes STR as a permitted use in all residential and Village mixed-use zones with no commercial/industrial use, listing seven prohibited unit types — and containing no R-6/R-10 carve-out at all. Either the restriction postdates the 2023 memo, or the widely repeated WarwickPost / BNBCalc / Yahoo-syndicated claim is wrong. No municode ordinance text confirming it could be located. City of Warwick memo (PDF)
  • North Kingstown RI — re-confirmed: NO adopted local STR ordinance. Town planning staff have been “researching” the issue since at least January 2023 with nothing adopted as of September 2026; only the statewide DBR registration and the new state tax apply. This matters — North Kingstown RI holds the two closest-to-station Section 2 candidates in the sweep. RI REALTORS · The Independent
  • Warren RI — cap: final adoption still unconfirmed by primary source. Secondary coverage (EastBayRI headline “Warren's new law caps density and total number of short-term rentals”) implies passage of a 125-unit cap, $100/unit fee, max 50% of units per building as STR, and one required off-street parking space — but the article body returned empty on fetch and the town's own ordinance page could not be retrieved, so no adoption date or vote count is confirmed. This is the second consecutive run it has not resolved, and it matters because Warren RI is again the deepest duplex market.
