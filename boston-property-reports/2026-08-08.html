Short-term rental rules by town

  • Narragansett RI — unsettled. The 2024 ordinance capping STR permits (1,100 initially, stepping toward 900 by September 2026) and banning stays under 7 nights was challenged by Narragansett 2100; in October 2024 Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted a restraining order and preliminary injunction. As of this August 2026 search no source indicates the litigation has been resolved — the ordinance remains enjoined and unenforced and the case is still described as ongoing. Treat Narragansett STR legality as unsettled and confirm current court status with an RI real-estate attorney before closing. Boston Globe · WPRI
  • South Kingstown RI (incl. Wakefield and Peace Dale) — Short-Term Residential Rental Registry adopted April 2024. Registration with the Building Official required before letting; $100 annual fee due December 31, $300 late fee, fines to $500; lease and registration must be posted at the unit entrance. No ban and no permit cap. Town notice
  • Jamestown RI — Registration required since August 2023: $350/year, building inspection every 3 years and fire marshal every 2, occupancy capped at 2 per bedroom plus 2, one parking space per bedroom plus one, and a local RI-resident contact who must respond within 2 hours. Three or more violations in 12 months can revoke the permit. No ban. Jamestown Press
  • Warwick RI — Comprehensive ordinance passed February 2023: STRs are a permitted use in residential and mixed-use zones and prohibited in commercial and industrial zones. Online registration, owner affidavit, smoke/CO detectors and extinguishers, no outdoor fires, grills 10 ft from structures, and guest records retained 180 days. No cap, no ban. WarwickPost
  • North Kingstown RI (incl. Wickford and Saunderstown) — no dedicated STR registration ordinance found as of this search. Council members have publicly discussed wanting oversight but nothing confirmed adopted. Verify directly with North Kingstown town hall before purchase; this could change. The Independent
  • Statewide Rhode Island — 5% whole-home STR tax: confirmed still in force. Effective January 1, 2026, RI imposes a 5% state tax on short-term rental of a residential dwelling rented in its entirety, separate from the existing 5% state hotel tax and on top of the local lodging tax, which itself doubled to 2% for 2026. Combined state and local tax on a whole-home weekly rental can now reach roughly 13–14%. Factor this directly into income projections. RI Division of Taxation notice
