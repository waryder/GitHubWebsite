STR rules by town — re-verified this run

  • Narragansett RI permit cap — STILL ENJOINED, NO FINAL RULING. In October 2024 RI Superior Court Judge Sarah Taft-Carter granted a preliminary injunction blocking enforcement, in a suit brought by the landlord group "Narragansett 2100" on home-rule / licensing-authority grounds. The ordinance — a permit cap starting at 1,100 and stepping down to 900 by September 2026, plus a 7-night minimum stay — remains on the books but unenforceable. No final ruling and no case resolution surfaced this run; the litigation is still open. Sources: Boston Globe, WPRI, Independent RI.
  • RI statewide 5% whole-home STR tax — CONFIRMED FROM THE PRIMARY SOURCE for a second consecutive run. Effective 2026-01-01, a 5% Whole Home Short-Term Rental Tax applies to rentals of 30 days or fewer of entire residential dwellings, including houses, condos and mobile homes. Enacted via the FY2026 budget, House Resolution 5076 Sub A, Article 5 §6. The local hotel tax rises from 1% to 2% on the same date, confirmed in the same document's filing-obligation table. No stay is double-taxed with both the 5% single-room hotel tax and the 5% whole-home tax. Primary source: RI Division of Taxation FAQ (PDF, Dec 2025); corroborated by RI Association of Realtors.
  • South Kingstown RI registry — confirmed, with a nuance now worth separating. There are two distinct actions, not one: a Short-Term Residential Rental Registry adopted 2024-04-08, and a broader Residential Rental Registration ordinance covering all rental dwellings adopted 2025-10-14 ($100 registration fee, $300 late fee). The registration deadline was extended from 2025-12-31 to 2026-03-31. Search summaries routinely blend the two into a single ordinance — they are related but separate and should be tracked as such. Sources: South Kingstown Rental Registration page, CivicAlert.
  • Warwick RI R-6 / R-10 non-owner-occupied bar — SECONDARY-SOURCE-ONLY for a third consecutive run. Secondary sources consistently state STRs are prohibited in the R-6 and R-10 residential zones (mostly single-family neighborhoods) and permitted in other residential and mixed-use village zones, under a February 2023 ordinance. Municode was not re-tested this run — it has been JS-gated and unscrapable in prior runs. Treat as likely true but not primary-confirmed. Sources: Warwick Post, City of Warwick STR memo to the RI STR Commission (PDF).
  • Jamestown RI — confirmed. Registration with the town clerk required; $350 annual fee; building-official inspection every 3 years and fire-marshal inspection every 2 years. No unit cap found. Sources: Jamestown Press.
  • Barrington RI — confirmed. Ordinance No. 2022-7, effective 2022-05-02: registration with the Barrington RI Police Department and a local contact reachable 24/7. No cap and no ban found. Source: eCode360.
  • North Kingstown RI — UNRESOLVED. No adopted STR ordinance or registry confirmed. The most recent sourcing shows the town council only beginning to "mull" a registry, with 54 registered Airbnbs at the time of reporting. Verify directly with North Kingstown RI town hall before assuming either a rule or the absence of one. Source: Independent RI. Note the exposure: 25 Thomas St North Kingstown RI is the single closest-to-station candidate in the whole sweep, and its STR regime is the least settled.
  • Warren RI — partially confirmed, final adoption unclear. The Town Council gave unanimous first passage to an ordinance that would cap total STR units townwide, require annual registration, and mandate one off-street parking space per unit. Second-passage / final adoption could not be confirmed, so the cap's status is open. Source: EastBayRI. Warren RI is this run's deepest duplex market, which makes the unresolved cap materially relevant.
  • Narragansett RI duplex "ban" — re-verified, still UNADOPTED, and more tangled than previously recorded. Two distinct threads, both unadopted. (1) April 2024: town officials sought to ban duplexes townwide except by special permit in two zones, reported as an attempt to work around the state's pro-density zoning law (Providence Journal via PressReader, 2024-04-17). (2) 2025: the Town Council introduced 14 zoning amendments, some characterized in reporting as loosening duplex restrictions; the Planning Board recommended against the package and, amid resident opposition, the Council withdrew the amendments to rework them — no vote taken, workshops planned but not yet scheduled (Independent RI, WPRI). The two threads point in opposite directions — one restricting, one loosening — and are flagged as a conflict rather than resolved. Net: no adopted rule either way.

  • Source gating flipped this run. Last run Zillow and Redfin were JS-gated on all three sweeps simultaneously. Estimated impact: roughly 2–3 of ~25 corridor listings carry a material data gap traceable to gating — principally 0 Oak Street Attleboro MA's package price and acreage, 145 Winthrop St Rehoboth MA's canonical listing page, and 23 Colonial Way Rehoboth MA's price and MLS number.
  • All drive times on this page are estimates derived from road geography, not from a routing API. Verify anything decision-critical in Google or Apple Maps.
  • Norwood Hospital — re-confirmed closed, with significant new detail. Closed since June 2020 flooding; reconstruction stalled through Steward Health Care's 2024 bankruptcy. On 2026-08-07 Governor Healey signed an eminent-domain bill (H.5553 as amended by S.3231) letting the state, via DCAMM, seize the 11-acre property from Medical Properties Trust if MPT does not reach a deal with a qualified nonprofit hospital operator by 2026-11-01. Two operators were reported in active talks as of early August; Mass General Brigham explored acquiring the site but MPT raised its ask from $250M to $375M and MGB says there are no conversations at this time. The hospital is non-operational today and no reopening is expected before the November deadline resolves. Net effect: the 15-minute hospital rule still fails corridor-wide for Canton MA, Stoughton MA, Sharon MA and Norwood MA, with only Attleboro MA and Rehoboth MA covered via Sturdy Memorial. Sources: WBUR, WBUR, Boston.com.
