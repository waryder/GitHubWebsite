Get your hands into it.

  • Sculpt: grab the metal with a finger or mouse and pull. Release it to let the surface settle back.
  • Orbit: switch modes, then drag to turn the view. Pinch or scroll to zoom in either mode.
  • Split / Fuse: separate the sculpture into droplets, then let them flow together.
  • Finishes: switch between chrome, gold, oil slick, and obsidian.
  • Studio: add droplets, change lighting and flow speed, save a picture, or reset.
  • Keyboard: focus the sculpture and use arrows to turn it, + / − to zoom, and Space to freeze. The Studio’s Stir button reshapes it without dragging.

A procedural sculpture, not a scientific fluid simulation. Everything is generated on your device; there are no uploads or external libraries. Reloading starts a fresh sculpture.
